# NemoStation/Marlin-2B on NVIDIA A100 SXM 80GB (HGX 8-GPU; no FP8/FP4 tensor cores)

Research date: **2026-09-19**. Formulas, legend and rules per
[`research/METHODOLOGY.md`](../../METHODOLOGY.md). Model facts from
[`models/marlin2b/architecture.md`](architecture.md); hardware facts from
[`gpus/a100.md`](../../gpus/a100.md); prices from
[`cross-cutting/cloud-pricing.md`](../../cross-cutting/cloud-pricing.md) §5.2 / §8.

> **Naming collision, stated once.** "Marlin" is also the name of vLLM's INT4/FP8 W4A16
> mixed-input GEMM kernel, and that kernel is a first-class A100 citizen
> ([`gpus/a100.md`](../../gpus/a100.md) §6). Every occurrence below is disambiguated as
> **Marlin-2B** (the model) or **Marlin kernel** (the GEMM).

---

## 0. Verdict

1. **Runnable today: yes, but not as shipped.** `transformers >= 5.7.0` with
   `trust_remote_code=True` is the only first-class path; **vLLM ≥ 0.17.0** (current 0.29.0) and
   **SGLang ≥ 0.5.9** (current 0.5.20) serve the weights only after
   `--hf-overrides '{"architectures": ["Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration"]}'`, because
   `MarlinForConditionalGeneration` is in no engine registry (architecture.md §8.1, §8.4;
   [inference-engines.md §6.5](../../cross-cutting/inference-engines.md)).
2. **Min GPUs 1, recommended 1 — `--tensor-parallel-size 1`, scale out by data-parallel replicas.**
   4.12–5.07 GiB of weights against 72 GiB usable; TP > 1 is *harmful* here (§1), multi-node is
   never needed, no fabric question.
3. **Weight format actually executed: BF16 native.** A100 (sm_80) has **no FP8 and no FP4 tensor
   cores**; the only published narrower checkpoints are INT4-W4A16 GPTQ and INT8, both of which run
   (Marlin kernel / CUTLASS INT8) but are unevaluated for timestamp accuracy (§2, §6).
4. **Cost per 1M output tokens (S1, 4K in / 512 out, TPOT ≤ 50 ms, batch 256):
   $0.022 (low) – $0.048 (high)** `est.`; calibrated against the one A100 measurement available,
   **$0.033 – $0.071**. Max-throughput (S4, batch 887 = KV ceiling): **$0.019 – $0.041** `est.`
   **A 2-minute video caption costs $0.0008–0.0018.**
5. **Confidence: `estimate`.** No Marlin-2B measurement exists on any hardware; A100 is absent from
   MLPerf v5.0/v5.1/v6.0 and from InferenceMAX; the single usable proxy (InternVL3.5-2B on A100
   40GB, vLLM issue #24728) implies an effective decode MBU of **≈ 0.47**, ~1.5× below the
   planning default, and that haircut is carried through §3–§4.

### 1.2 Which tensors cannot be sharded — and why TP > 1 is a trap here

| Tensor group | Params | Shardable? | Consequence on A100 |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `self_attn.{q,k,v,o}_proj`, 6 layers | 314.6 M | head-parallel, **but `num_key_value_heads = 2`** | **At TP > 2 vLLM replicates KV heads to `tp_size`.** Aggregate KV memory then grows linearly with TP while per-GPU KV stays flat — TP=8 stores **4× the KV bytes** of TP=1 for the same traffic. This is the single reason TP > 2 is wrong here. |
| `linear_attn.in_proj_{qkv,z,a,b}`, `out_proj`, 18 layers | ~1,058 M | value-head-parallel, 16 heads → TP ∈ {1,2,4,8,16} divide | `in_proj_b` / `in_proj_a` are `[16, 2048]` — **16 output elements in total.** Under an INT4 GPTQ build the Marlin kernel's `MIN_THREAD_N = 64` rejects the per-rank slice ([vLLM #35924](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/35924), reported failing for Qwen3.5-27B at **TP ≥ 1**). BF16 is unaffected. |
| `linear_attn.conv1d.weight` (depthwise, 18×[6144,1,4]) | 442 k | channel-parallel only | **not quantisable** (architecture.md §3.6) |
| `linear_attn.A_log`, `dt_bias`, `norm`; `input_layernorm`, `post_attention_layernorm`, `q_norm`, `k_norm`, `model.norm` | 106 k | **replicated on every rank** | 0.55 M params ≈ 1.10 MB/GPU — negligible, but it is the term that stops `weights/n_gpus` being exact |
| `embed_tokens` + materialised `lm_head` (248,320 × 2,048 each) | 508.6 M ×2 stored | vocab-parallel | the duplicate is 1.017 GB of pure waste; a tie-honouring loader drops it |
| **Entire vision tower** (24-block ViT + merger) | 331.4 M | vLLM runs it **data-parallel** with `--mm-encoder-tp-mode data`, i.e. **replicated per rank** | at TP=8 the encoder is 8× resident and 8× as many patches are needed to fill it |

**Non-shardable in the METHODOLOGY §3 sense:** the norms, `A_log`/`dt_bias`, and (under
`--mm-encoder-tp-mode data`) the whole 331 M-param vision tower. Everything else shards, and none
of it needs to.

### 1.3 Fit table — HGX A100 8-GPU topology {1, 2, 4, 8, 16}

`usable_hbm` **67.06 GiB = 72.0 GB/GPU** (decimal basis, resolved 2026-09-19 — §1.1),
activation workspace 4 GiB/GPU, weights **as shipped** 5.0698 GiB,
`max_concurrency(ctx) = floor(kv_budget_per_gpu / (ctx × kv_bytes_per_token_per_gpu + fixed_state/tp))`.
All `est.`, computed in `python3`:

```
GPUs    par  W/GPU GiB  KVbudget GiB  KV B/tok/GPU      8K     32K    128K     1M |   8K f8  32K f8 128K f8  1M f8
   1    TP1      5.070         57.99        12,288     517     147      38      4 |     891     281      75      9
   2    TP2      2.535         60.52         6,144   1,081     307      79     10 |   1,860     588     157     20
   4    TP4      1.267         61.79         6,144   1,201     321      81     10 |   2,207     628     162     20
   8    TP8      0.634         62.42         6,144   1,270     328      82     10 |   2,427     650     165     20
  16    TP16     0.317         62.74         6,144   1,306     332      83     10 |   2,552     661     166     20
       (2 nodes)
```

**Read the TP2 → TP8 columns carefully.** Doubling from 1 to 2 GPUs doubles concurrency (2.09×) —
that is the honest scaling, because at TP ≤ 2 the two KV heads split cleanly. From TP2 to TP8, four
times the silicon buys **17 % more concurrency**, because the KV heads are replicated and
`kv_bytes_per_token_per_gpu` stops falling. **8 independent TP1 replicas give 8 × 517 = 4,136
concurrent 8K sequences against TP8's 1,270** — a 3.3× capacity difference for the same node, on
top of removing all NVLink all-reduce traffic. This is the whole parallelism answer for this pair.

**Multi-node fabric: never needed.** The TP16 row is arithmetic only — it would cross an
InfiniBand/EFA hop (200 Gb/s per GPU on DGX A100, 4×100 Gb/s EFA on `p4de`, i.e. ~24× below
intra-node NVLink, [`gpus/a100.md`](../../gpus/a100.md) §4) to serve a model that fits 14× over in
one GPU. `HGX A100 4-GPU has no NVSwitch` (direct NVLink only) and is equally irrelevant here.

**Sensitivity — the two variants the primary table does *not* use:**

```
[A] optimistic usable = 0.90 x 80 GiB = 72.0 GiB   [nvidia-smi 81,920 MiB reading]
    *** ⚠️ TO BE VERIFIED, +7.4 %, NOT the planning basis (gpus/a100.md section2) ***
  TP1: budget 62.93 GiB  ->  8K 562  32K 160  128K 41  1M 5
  TP2: budget 65.47 GiB  ->  8K 1,169  32K 332  128K 86  1M 10
  TP4: budget 66.73 GiB  ->  8K 1,297  32K 347  128K 88  1M 11
  TP8: budget 67.37 GiB  ->  8K 1,370  32K 354  128K 89  1M 11

[B] TP1 with tie-honoured weights 4.1224 GiB (loader that honours tie_word_embeddings),
    on the primary decimal basis
  W/GPU 4.1225 GiB   budget 58.93 GiB   8K 526  32K 149  128K 38  1M 4
  ...and on [A]'s 72.0 GiB basis: budget 63.88 GiB  ->  8K 570
```

The spread across both variants is 517 → 570 at 8K, i.e. **±5 %**. Neither materially changes
a deployment decision; both are stated so the arithmetic reproduces. **The capacity half of that
spread is now resolved** (§1.1): the primary table is the decimal one; [A] is retained only as a
labelled sensitivity.

**Video-native contexts** (the only ones a Marlin-2B fleet actually sees — the 240-frame cap fixes
every video request at ~23.5 K tokens regardless of clip length, architecture.md §6.3), TP1:

## 3. Throughput and latency

### 3.1 Assumptions (METHODOLOGY §4)

| Quantity | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|

| **MBU (decode), measured-calibrated** | **0.47** | derived in §3.3 from the only A100 measurement of a 2B-class VLM under vLLM |
| MFU, prefill GEMM | 0.40 | METHODOLOGY §4 band 0.35–0.50; architecture.md §10.3 uses 0.40 flat |
| MFU, attention | 0.30 | [`gpus/a100.md`](../../gpus/a100.md) §9 worked examples use 0.45 GEMM / 0.30 attention |
| Comm overhead | **0 %** | TP1 |
| `ctx` convention | **prompt + output** | end-of-generation, so the TPOT ≤ 50 ms SLO holds for the whole request |
| Peak dense BF16 | 312 TFLOPS | never the sparse 624 |

Formulas, verbatim from METHODOLOGY §4:
`bytes_per_decode_step(batch) = 3,763,650,176 + batch × ctx × 12,288` (dense model — all weights
read every step, regardless of batch);
`decode_step_time = max(bytes / (2.039e12 × MBU), 2 × 1,881,825,088 × batch / (312e12 × MFU))`;
`prefill_time(T) = 3.7637e9 × T / (312e12 × 0.40) + 24,576 × T² / (312e12 × 0.30)`.

### 4.6 Comparison to the vendor API, and break-even

**There is no vendor API for Marlin-2B.** architecture.md §11.1 checked and found
`inferenceProviderMapping: {}` on the Hub, a Gradio demo at `vlm.nemostation.com` with no
token-priced endpoint, and a services (custom fine-tuning) monetisation model. The base
`Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B` has one provider (`featherless-ai`) but **text-only, not video**. So
METHODOLOGY §6's "compare to the vendor's published API price" has **no anchor**, and the
structured output's `vendor_api_output_usd_per_1m` is `null`. ⚠️ Whether Fireworks, Together,
Replicate, Baseten, DeepInfra or SiliconFlow host it, and at what price, remains open
(architecture.md §12 #22).

**Break-even is therefore a utilisation question, not an API question.** At the `low` tier and
1,902 two-minute videos/h:

```
duty  100%: $0.8360 per 1k videos  ($0.000836/video)
duty   50%: $1.6719 per 1k videos  ($0.001672/video)
duty   25%: $3.3438 per 1k videos  ($0.003344/video)
duty   10%: $8.3596 per 1k videos  ($0.008360/video)
```

A rented A100 becomes worse than **a hypothetical $0.01/video managed endpoint** only below ~8 %
duty cycle; above that, self-hosting on the cheapest A100 tier wins by more than 10×. The
practical break-even is therefore **not against an API price but against an H100**: at the `low`
tiers, `h100` costs 2.01× more per GPU-hour ($3.20 vs $1.59, cloud-pricing.md §8) and delivers
**2.88× the sustained video throughput** (architecture.md §11.2: 6,899 vs 2,394 videos/h), so
**H100 is cheaper per video than A100 at both the `low` and `high` tiers.** A100's case here is
capital already owned, or the **$0.70–$1.00/GPU-hour all-in colo rate** for used silicon
([`gpus/a100.md`](../../gpus/a100.md) §8), which puts 2-min videos at **$0.37–$0.53 per 1,000**
and beats every rented tier on any GPU in the roster.

## 5. Scaling and deployment shape

**Single node, single GPU, replicas.** The deployment unit is **one A100 running TP1**, and the
scaling unit is another one. An HGX A100 8-GPU node runs **8 independent Marlin-2B replicas**
behind a round-robin router; there is no tensor-parallel cut worth making (§1.2), no PP, no EP, no
multi-node fabric, and NVLink carries nothing but the occasional NCCL health check.

**When PD or wide-EP pays for this pair: never, with one caveat.** The model is dense (no EP at
all), and a 1.4 s prefill against a 5.8 s decode on the *same* GPU is not an imbalance worth a KV
transfer. The caveat is **SGLang's EPD (Encode-Prefill-Decode) disaggregation**, which separates
the **vision encoder** from the language model with Mooncake as the transfer backend
([serving-optimizations.md §3](../../cross-cutting/serving-optimizations.md),
[src](https://kvcache-ai.github.io/Mooncake/)). Marlin-2B's ViT is **39 % of prefill compute**
(65.32 of 167.4 TFLOP per 2-min clip, architecture.md §6.4) and is pure dense GEMM at
`hidden 1024`, so an encode pool is architecturally sensible. ⚠️ **Unverified on sm_80 and
unverified for `qwen3_5` video ingestion in SGLang at all** (architecture.md §12 #15) — do not plan
on it.

**Launch commands.**

vLLM — the remap flag is documented API; the supported-models page gives the same shape for other
models [src](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/models/supported_models.html), and `hf_overrides` is a
first-class `ModelConfig` field
[src](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vllm-project/vllm/main/vllm/config/model.py):

```shell
vllm serve NemoStation/Marlin-2B --port 8000 \
  --tensor-parallel-size 1 \
  --hf-overrides '{"architectures": ["Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration"]}' \
  --max-model-len 32768 \
  --mamba-cache-mode=align \
  --no-enable-prefix-caching \
  --kv-cache-dtype fp8_e4m3 \
  --max-num-seqs 256 \
  --max-cudagraph-capture-size 256 \
  --mm-processor-cache-type shm \
  --media-io-kwargs '{"video": {"num_frames": -1}}'
```

Flag provenance, one by one: `--hf-overrides` and `--media-io-kwargs '{"video": {"num_frames":
-1}}'` from architecture.md §8.1/§8.6; `--mamba-cache-mode=align` is **mandatory** —
`mamba_cache_mode == "all"` raises `NotImplementedError` for Qwen3.5
[src](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vllm-project/vllm/main/vllm/model_executor/models/qwen3_5.py);
`--mm-processor-cache-type shm` from the vLLM Qwen3.5 recipe
[src](https://docs.vllm.ai/projects/recipes/en/latest/Qwen/Qwen3.5.html);
`--max-cudagraph-capture-size` lowered from its 512 default because of the documented
*"causal_conv1d_update assertion when cuda graph capture size is larger than mamba cache size"*
(same recipe); `--kv-cache-dtype fp8_e4m3` per [`gpus/a100.md`](../../gpus/a100.md) §6
(*"the single highest-leverage A100 flag"*); prefix caching **off** per §2. `--mm-encoder-tp-mode
data` is omitted deliberately — it is a no-op at TP1.

Optional GPU video decode (the ⚠️ path, §2), requires `nvidia-cuda-mps-control -d` first
[src](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/stable/features/multimodal_inputs/):

```shell
  --media-io-kwargs '{"video": {"backend": "pynvvideocodec", "num_frames": -1}}' \
  --mm-ipc-gpu-memory-gb 2
```

SGLang (same remap needed; base-model recipe shape from architecture.md §8.6):

```shell
python -m sglang.launch_server --model-path <marlin-2b-with-remapped-architectures> --port 8000 \
  --tp-size 1 --mem-fraction-static 0.8 --context-length 32768
```

`transformers` — the only first-class path, and the one the author's own Space uses
(`attn_implementation="sdpa"`, Path A env vars set explicitly; architecture.md §8.6):

```bash
pip install "transformers>=5.7.0" "torch>=2.11.0" torchcodec "qwen-vl-utils>=0.0.14" av pillow
```

**What you must re-supply under either engine** (architecture.md §1.3, §1.4, §6.3): both EOS ids
`[248044, 248046]`; the `<think>`-prefix strip; the three canonical prompts; and the Path A
frame/pixel budget, or you silently get Path B's half-resolution input.

**Warm-up and loading.** Weights are **5,443,677,224 B on disk**; over NVMe:

```
 5 GB/s -> 1.09 s
10 GB/s -> 0.54 s
20 GB/s -> 0.27 s
```

Weight load is a rounding error. **The warm-up cost that matters on A100 is elsewhere:**

- **Triton GDN autotune.** A100 is on the Triton/FLA GDN prefill path (§2), and
  [vLLM #36598](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/36598) documents the failure mode on
  **non-SM90 GPUs**: `_forward_core` returns early during the V1 profile run, so the autotuner
  never runs before KV cache allocation, and **the first real request OOMs**. Fix proposed in
  [PR #43047](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/43047); ⚠️ not confirmed merged. Budget a
  warm-up request and leave `--gpu-memory-utilization` headroom.
- **CUDA-graph capture** at `--max-cudagraph-capture-size 256`, and capture allocates *outside*
  `--gpu-memory-utilization` ([serving-optimizations.md §4.2](../../cross-cutting/serving-optimizations.md)).
- **`torch.compile`** on first call if `marlin.compile()` is used under `transformers`.

Total cold start: **tens of seconds**, dominated by compile/capture/autotune, not by the 5.4 GB.

**The I/O budget nobody prices.** At the §4.4 throughputs one A100 must sustain:

```
2-min clips, 2048 out   1,902 vid/h ->  126.8 decoded 448x448 frames/s
2-min clips,  768 out   2,285 vid/h ->  152.3 decoded frames/s
30 s clips,   768 out   9,390 vid/h ->  156.5 decoded frames/s
```

Five NVDEC units on the A100 handle that comfortably; CPU torchcodec at 448×448 ⚠️ is unmeasured
and is the most likely real production bottleneck (architecture.md §12 #6). Plan the host CPU, or
turn on `pynvvideocodec`.

## 6. Risks and open questions

> **Closed 2026-09-19 — A100 usable-HBM basis.** This was an open question here
> (§1.1's "two disagreements"). It is now **resolved to 80 GB decimal**:
> `usable_hbm` **72.0 GB = 67.06 GiB/GPU**, per [METHODOLOGY §8](../../METHODOLOGY.md#gpus)
> and [`gpus/a100.md` §2](../../gpus/a100.md). The 80 GiB (`nvidia-smi` 81,920 MiB)
> reading this document used to plan on is unsourced, is flagged ⚠️ **TO BE
> VERIFIED** in `gpus/a100.md` §2, and survives only as §1.3's labelled +7.4 %
> sensitivity. §1.3's KV budget is **57.99 GiB** at TP1 with this document's
> 4 GiB activation workspace; [`architecture.md`](architecture.md) §8.5 prints
> **58.9 GiB** for the same basis because it uses a 3 GiB workspace — same
> capacity basis, different workspace assumption, not a disagreement.
> The remaining ±5 % spread in §1.3 is the *weights* question (tie-honoured vs
> as-shipped `lm_head`), which is open — see risk 1.

**Blocking — resolve before committing A100 capacity**

1. ⚠️ **The `--hf-overrides` remap has never been run end to end.** Strong evidence
   (`class MarlinForConditionalGeneration(Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration)` with an unmodified
   forward pass, a third party already shipping the remap, documented vLLM API) but no confirmed
   load. Sub-question: how vLLM handles the materialised `lm_head.weight` against
   `tie_word_embeddings: true` (architecture.md §8.1, §12 #2). **A100 adds nothing to this risk and
   removes nothing from it.**
2. ⚠️ **Path A (23,520 tokens) vs Path B (12,288 tokens).** The gated `processor_config.json`
   confirms the 1.91× conflict rather than resolving it. Serving through vLLM/SGLang likely halves
   the frames or the resolution silently. On A100 this is worth **~40 % of $/video** (§4.5) —
   which makes it tempting, and it is a *quality* decision, not a cost one (architecture.md §6.3,
   §12 #1).
3. ⚠️ **Triton GDN autotuner OOM on non-SM90 GPUs** — [vLLM #36598](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/36598).
   A100 is squarely in the affected class. Fix proposed ([#43047](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/43047));
   merge status unconfirmed. **This is the most likely way an A100 Marlin-2B deployment dies on its
   first production request.**
4. ⚠️ **The 240-frame cap on long video.** A 10-minute clip is sampled at 0.40 fps and an hour at
   0.067 fps for constant prefill cost (§4.4). Whether "second-precise timestamps" survive is
   unmeasured — and A100's low $/video makes chunking client-side cheap, so there is no economic
   excuse not to.

**A100-specific, high impact**

5. ⚠️ **The GDN prefill penalty is unquantified.** No FlashQLA on sm_80; Triton/FLA instead. The
   vendor claim is 2–3× on the GDN forward pass. §4.5 brackets $/1k videos at $0.84–$1.45 across a
   1.0×–2.0× prefill penalty — a **74 % spread on the headline cost number**. Closing this needs one
   profiled prefill on real hardware. **Correcting architecture.md §8.3:** the penalty is
   *prefill-only*; GDN **decode** gets vLLM's fused CUDA kernel at cc 8.0+
   ([flash-attention.md §14.3](../../cross-cutting/flash-attention.md)).
6. ⚠️ **Effective MBU is ≈ 0.47, not 0.70.** Derived in §3.4 from one proxy on a *different* 2B VLM
   on the *40 GB* part. Two SLO rows flip on it. Needs a real Marlin-2B measurement.
7. ⚠️ **No FP8 anywhere.** Not a format risk (there is no FP8 Marlin-2B checkpoint) but it removes
   every published Ampere workaround from the table: [vLLM #54318](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/54318)
   shows the family's FP8 builds failing outright on 4× A100 with `fp8e4nv unsupported in SM80`.
   Do not let a future FP8 Marlin-2B build be assumed to run here.
8. ⚠️ **Video decode throughput at 127–157 frames/s** (§5) is unmeasured on CPU, and the vLLM
   NVDEC/`pynvvideocodec` result was measured on **H100 only**. If the bottleneck is I/O, every
   number in §3–§4 is an upper bound.
9. ⚠️ **No A100 profile exists for any model in this repo** on any engine's recipe catalogue
   ([inference-engines.md §3.2](../../cross-cutting/inference-engines.md): `hw/a100.json` → 404 on
   all three large models), and **SGLang's `sgl_kernel` wheel has shipped without an sm_80 cubin
   before** ([sglang #11421](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/issues/11421)). Verify the sm_80
   binary before rollout.

**Accuracy caveats of the executed precision**

10. **BF16 is the checkpoint's native dtype** — no quantisation error at all on the recommended
    path. This is the one place A100 is *safer* than a Blackwell FP4 deployment.
11. ⚠️ **If you quantise to fit more replicas per node, you are the first person to evaluate it.**
    architecture.md §9: **not one** of the GPTQ-INT4, SDNQ-INT8, MLX-8bit or GGUF repos publishes a
    CaReBench / DREAM-1K / TimeLens score, a perplexity delta, or any accuracy measurement. For a
    model whose product is second-precise timestamps, **numeric error landing on the timestamp
    digits is a specific, plausible and entirely unmeasured failure mode.** INT4 W4A16 on A100 saves
    ~2 GB of a 67 GiB budget — buy nothing, risk the product.
12. ⚠️ **FP8 KV is storage-only on A100** (no FA3 ⇒ no FP8 attention math, and **no per-head
    scales**, only per-tensor). It is the right default here for the 2× concurrency, but calibrate
    the scales with llm-compressor — the default all-1.0 scales are the accuracy-risky option
    ([`gpus/a100.md`](../../gpus/a100.md) §6) — and re-check timestamps after enabling it.
13. ⚠️ **Engine-vs-`transformers` quality has never been compared** for this model on any GPU
    (architecture.md §12 #3): fixed training prompt, `<think>` artefact, two-element EOS list,
    bespoke preprocessing. Four places an engine can diverge silently.

**Second-order**

14. ⚠️ **A100 power SKU lottery.** MLPerf v3.1 shows a 500 W CTS A100 beating a 400 W A100 by
    **1.56×** on the identical benchmark and stack ([`gpus/a100.md`](../../gpus/a100.md) §10, §11).
    When renting you usually cannot tell which you got. Run `nvidia-smi -q -d POWER` and record the
    enforced limit alongside every number, or your A/B is noise.
15. ⚠️ **Clock throttling changes which kernel wins** — Marlin's own study found reduced clocks harm
    prior kernels but not Marlin ([src](https://github.com/IST-DASLab/marlin)). Benchmark at locked
    base clock in a dense 8-GPU chassis.
16. ⚠️ **`in_proj_b`/`in_proj_a` `MIN_THREAD_N = 64`** under an INT4 GPTQ build at TP > 1
    ([vLLM #35924](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/35924)). Irrelevant at the
    recommended TP1; a landmine if someone "scales up" by sharding.
17. ⚠️ **No hosted API and no price anchor** (§4.6). Every cost conclusion here is a
    cost-of-goods figure with no market to check it against.
18. ⚠️ **`.multi_find` production cost** — N sequential ffmpeg `libx264` re-encodes plus N full
    prefills, CPU work the GPU roofline does not see (architecture.md §1.4, §12 #11). On a
    prefill-bound pair (§4.3) this multiplies the expensive phase by N.
19. **Driver/CUDA floor is rising.** Dynamo requires driver ≥ 580 and CUDA 13.0/13.1; CUDA 13.x
    removed everything before Turing but **kept sm_80** ([`gpus/a100.md`](../../gpus/a100.md) §1,
    §11). A100 is supported, not deprecated — but it is on the oldest supported rung.
