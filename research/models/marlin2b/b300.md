# NemoStation/Marlin-2B on NVIDIA B300 Blackwell Ultra (HGX 8-GPU, 268GB per GPU as deployed, 2,144GB per node; this repo's primary node)

Research date **2026-09-19**. Formulas, legend (`[src]` / `est.` / `meas.` / ⚠️ **TO BE VERIFIED**)
and the pinned inputs are [`research/METHODOLOGY.md`](../../METHODOLOGY.md) §§1–8. Model facts come
from [`models/marlin2b/architecture.md`](architecture.md); hardware facts from
[`gpus/b300.md`](../../gpus/b300.md); prices **only** from
[`cross-cutting/cloud-pricing.md`](../../cross-cutting/cloud-pricing.md) §5.14, cited by row name.
Every derived number in this document was produced by one `python3` script whose output is pasted
verbatim into the tables below.

**Pinned inputs used (METHODOLOGY §8, no deviations):** B300 HBM **268 GB as deployed**
(2,144 GB per 8-GPU node), HBM BW **8.0 TB/s**, dense **BF16 2,250 / FP8 4,500 / NVFP4 13,500
TFLOPS**, MBU **0.60**, MFU **0.40**. Marlin-2B **2,213,241,664 unique params**, **5.444 GB on
disk / 4.426 GB BF16 resident**, **12,288 B/token BF16 KV** (6 of 24 layers), **19,537,920 B
(18.63 MiB) GDN state per sequence** at **S = 1** slot.

**Input-consistency check.** METHODOLOGY §8, [`gpus/b300.md`](../../gpus/b300.md) §2 and
[`cloud-pricing.md`](../../cross-cutting/cloud-pricing.md) §2 all state **268 GB / 2,144 GB** — no
disagreement to resolve. The only conflicting figure in the sources is NVIDIA's own
*"288 GB of HBM3e per GPU"*
[src](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/inside-nvidia-blackwell-ultra-the-chip-powering-the-ai-factory-era/),
which is the **physical per-die** capacity; `gpus/b300.md` §11 gotcha 1 explains why sizing against
it over-commits an HGX B300 by ~10 %. **288 GB is the GB300 NVL72 figure** (≈ 279 usable), not this
node. Likewise the compute: the same NVIDIA blog's *"15 petaFLOPS"* dense FP4 is the **GB300 NVL72
clock bin**; the HGX B300 bin this document plans with is **13,500 TFLOPS** dense FP4 and
**2,250 TFLOPS** dense BF16 — *identical BF16 to B200*
([`gpus/b300.md`](../../gpus/b300.md) §3, [`quantization-formats.md`](../../cross-cutting/quantization-formats.md) §4.3).

## 0. Verdict

1. **Runnable today: yes, with one flag — but "supported" is contested inside this repo.** The
   checkpoint's `architectures` string is unregistered in every engine, so you serve it as
   **vLLM ≥ v0.29.0** with `--hf-overrides '{"architectures": ["Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration"]}'`
   ([architecture.md §8.1](architecture.md)); [`inference-engines.md`](../../cross-cutting/inference-engines.md) §6.5,
   written before gate access, still says *"no engine supports it"* — that row is **superseded**, see §6.
   The **only first-class** path remains `transformers >= 5.7.0` + `trust_remote_code=True`.
2. **Min GPUs 1, recommended 1. Parallelism TP1; scale with DP replicas (8 per node).** Weights are
   4.426 GB against a 241 GB usable budget; TP > 2 *reduces* efficiency without adding KV capacity
   (only 2 KV heads to shard — §1).
3. **Weight format actually executed: BF16.** No FP8, NVFP4 or MXFP4 Marlin checkpoint exists
   ([architecture.md §9](architecture.md)), so **B300's entire FP4 differentiator is unused by this pair**.
   The one 4-bit option (`prasannaJagadesh/marlin-2B-GPTQ-4BITS`) is W4A16 — BF16 math rate, and it
   trips a live vLLM GDN kernel bug (§6).
4. **Interactive (S1, TPOT ≤ 50 ms): $0.022 – $0.045 per 1M output tokens** (`low` $7.40 → `high`
   $15.00); blended 75/25 with 50 % cached input **$0.0092 – $0.0185 / 1M**. **Max-throughput (S4) is
   the same operating point** — the 50 ms SLO never binds on a 2B hybrid model, KV capacity does.
   At the *video* operating point (23.5 K ctx): **$0.126 – $0.276 / 1M output**, **$0.000487 per
   2-minute video** at `low`.
5. **Confidence: `estimate`.** Zero published measurements exist for Marlin-2B on any GPU
   ([architecture.md §10.2](architecture.md)); the nearest same-GPU anchors are RunPod's vLLM 0.28/0.29
   Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen2.5-7B B300 runs, which land at **0.28–0.30× of their own roofline** at full
   batch — apply that haircut before promising anything (§3).

## 1. Fit

### 1.1 The one-line answer

`max_concurrency(ctx) = floor( (0.90 × 268e9 − 4.426e9 − 4 GiB) / (ctx × 12,288 + 19,537,920) )`
(METHODOLOGY §3). Script output:

```
usable HBM  = 241.2 GB   weights 4.426 GB   act_ws 4.295 GB
KV budget   = 232.48 GB = 216.5 GiB per GPU
```

The 216.5 GiB KV budget reproduces [architecture.md §8.5](architecture.md)'s B300 row exactly.

`est.` ⚠️ **The 1M row is arithmetic only.** `max_position_embeddings` is 262,144 with
`rope_type: default` and no YaRN/NTK entry — running at 1M needs scaling the checkpoint does not
ship ([architecture.md §5.3](architecture.md)). Do not plan on it.

The **23,520 row is the only one a video fleet ever occupies**: the 240-frame cap bounds every video
request at ~23.5 K tokens regardless of clip length ([architecture.md §6.3](architecture.md)), so
concurrency is a constant, not a distribution.

### 1.3 Fit across the GPU counts this topology allows

HGX B300 allows {1, 2, 4, 8} inside the NVLink domain and {16, 24, …} across InfiniBand. (The
18/36/72 counts belong to GB300 NVL72, a different part — not applicable here.) Two deployment
shapes, and they do **not** behave the same:

| n GPU | mode | weights/GPU | non-shardable replicated | KV/token/GPU | GDN state/seq/GPU | max_conc @23,520 | max_conc @128K | **node-wide @23,520** |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | TP1 (one replica) | 4.426 GB | 1.097 MB | 12,288 B | 18.63 MiB | 753 | 142 | 753 |
| 2 | TP2 (one replica) | 2.214 GB | 1.097 MB | 6,144 B | 9.32 MiB | 1,506 | 285 | 1,506 |
| 4 | TP4 (one replica) | 1.107 GB | 1.097 MB | 6,144 B | 4.66 MiB | 1,556 | 286 | 1,556 |
| 8 | TP8 (one replica) | 0.554 GB | 1.097 MB | 6,144 B | 2.33 MiB | 1,582 | 287 | 1,582 |
| 16 | TP16 (2 nodes) | 0.278 GB | 1.097 MB | 6,144 B | 1.16 MiB | 1,595 | 288 | 1,595 |
| 1 | DP1 = 1× TP1 | 4.426 GB | — | 12,288 B | 18.63 MiB | 753/replica | 142/replica | **753** |
| 2 | DP2 = 2× TP1 | 4.426 GB | — | 12,288 B | 18.63 MiB | 753/replica | 142/replica | **1,506** |
| 4 | DP4 = 4× TP1 | 4.426 GB | — | 12,288 B | 18.63 MiB | 753/replica | 142/replica | **3,012** |
| 8 | **DP8 = 8× TP1 (recommended)** | 4.426 GB | — | 12,288 B | 18.63 MiB | 753/replica | 142/replica | **6,024** |
| 16 | DP16 (2 nodes, no fabric needed) | 4.426 GB | — | 12,288 B | 18.63 MiB | 753/replica | 142/replica | **12,048** |

`est.` **Read the TP column carefully: TP saturates at TP2.** `num_key_value_heads` is **2**
([architecture.md §2.2](architecture.md)), so the KV cache shards two ways and no further — vLLM
replicates KV heads above that. TP4 buys **+3.3 %** concurrency over TP2 and TP16 buys **+5.9 %**,
while paying NVLink all-reduce on every one of 24 layers. **DP scales linearly and TP does not.**

**Tensors that cannot be sharded** (replicated on every rank, 1.097 MB total —
[architecture.md §3.6](architecture.md)):

| Tensor group | Params | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| `linear_attn.conv1d.weight` (18 × 24,576) | 442,368 | depthwise conv over the q‖k‖v channel axis |
| `input_layernorm` + `post_attention_layernorm` (48 × 2,048) | 98,304 | per-channel norm |
| `linear_attn.norm` (18 × 128) | 2,304 | RMSNormGated over `value_head_dim` |
| `self_attn.q_norm` + `k_norm` (12 × 256) | 3,072 | per-head norm |
| `linear_attn.A_log` + `dt_bias` (36 × 16) | 576 | SSM scalars, one per v-head |
| `model.norm` | 2,048 | final norm |

Three further sharding ceilings, all structural: **GDN v-heads = 16** (TP > 16 cannot split the
recurrent state), **full-attention KV heads = 2**, **ViT heads = 16**. The GDN `in_proj_a` /
`in_proj_b` projections have output dim **16** — see §6 for the live vLLM kernel bug this triggers
on *quantised* builds.

**Multi-node fabric: not needed at any scale.** A 2B model replicated per GPU has zero cross-GPU
traffic. The 1.6 TB/s of ConnectX-8 per node ([gpus/b300.md §4](../../gpus/b300.md)) is idle for
this pair; you are paying for it and cannot use it.

### 1.4 Activation workspace

4 GiB assumed (METHODOLOGY §3's 2–6 GB band), matching [architecture.md §8.5](architecture.md). The
one Marlin-specific pressure on it is the **ViT burst**: 240 frames × 784 patches = 94,080 ViT
tokens in a single varlen forward ([architecture.md §6.4](architecture.md)). At hidden 1024 and
24 blocks that is real transient memory, and it is per *concurrent prefill*, not per sequence.
⚠️ **TO BE VERIFIED:** the peak ViT activation footprint at 240 frames under vLLM's chunked-prefill
scheduler; nobody has published it. Method: run with `--max-num-batched-tokens` sweeps and watch
`nvidia-smi` peak reserved.

## 3. Throughput and latency

### 3.1 Assumptions (METHODOLOGY §4)

```
decode_step_time = max( (3.7637e9 + batch × ctx × 12,288) / (8.0e12 × 0.60),
                        2 × 1,881,825,088 × batch / (2.250e15 × 0.40) )
prefill_time(T, frames) = (3.7637e9 × T + 24,576 × T² + frames × 0.2722e12) / (2.250e15 × 0.40)
```

- **MBU 0.60**, the middle of METHODOLOGY §4's Blackwell band (0.5–0.7). No B300-specific MBU has
  been published by any engine ([gpus/b300.md §9](../../gpus/b300.md)) ⚠️.
- **MFU 0.40**, BF16. Anchor: FA4 hits 71 % of dense BF16 peak on B200 for *pure attention*
  [src](https://tridao.me/blog/2026/flash4/); end-to-end prefill is far lower.
- **Weight read is 3.7637 GB every step regardless of batch** — dense model, and the 331 M-param
  vision tower is **not** read during decode ([architecture.md §6.2](architecture.md)).
- All TFLOPS **dense**. B300 BF16 = 2,250, *identical to B200*.

`est.` throughout. Per METHODOLOGY §3's consistency rule, batch 256 at 128 K context exceeds
`max_concurrency` (142) and is printed as `infeasible (KV)`, never as a number. **Every other cell is
feasible, and every cell is memory-bound** — this model never reaches the compute roof on B300, which
is precisely why B300's FP4 advantage is inert here.

**The structural finding: METHODOLOGY §4's TPOT ≤ 50 ms SLO is never the binding constraint.** Even
at the maximum batch the KV budget allows, TPOT is 35.7 / 46.9 / 48.4 ms across S1/S2/S3. **S1 and
S4 are therefore the same operating point on this pair** — there is no latency headroom left to trade
for throughput, because capacity ran out first.

### 3.5 Explaining the gap between estimated and measured

The proxies let us calibrate the roofline rather than just wave at it. Rooflining the *same two
models RunPod measured*, with the same MBU/MFU this document uses:

| Model | Measured B300 peak | Roofline at batch 64 | at batch 512+ (compute-bound) | measured ÷ roofline |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Llama-3.1-8B | 16,855 tok/s | **16,872** | 56,040 | **1.00× at b=64, 0.30× at full batch** |
| Qwen2.5-7B | 16,769 tok/s | — | 59,055 | **0.28× at full batch** |

Two readings, and they bracket the truth:

- **Optimistic reading:** RunPod's "chat peak" landed almost exactly on the batch-64 roofline
  (16,855 vs 16,872 — within 0.1 %), which says the roofline is *accurate at the batch the harness
  actually reached*, and that the harness's request-rate ladder simply stopped there.
- **Conservative reading:** if the ladder *did* saturate the GPU, the engine realises only
  **0.28–0.30×** of the full-batch roofline, and every §3.2 number at high batch should be haircut
  accordingly.

Applying that band to Marlin's S1 KV-max point:

| | roofline | × 0.30 | × 0.45 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| S1, batch 256 | 73,808 tok/s | 22,142 | 33,214 |
| S1, batch 1,024 | 88,877 tok/s | 26,663 | 39,995 |
| S1, batch 3,327 (KV max) | 93,271 tok/s | 27,981 | 41,972 |

**Plan on 28,000–42,000 output tok/s/GPU at the S1 operating point, not 93,271** (`est.`, derived
from the proxy ratio above). Named sources of the gap, all specific to this pair:

1. **Engine overhead at extreme batch.** 3,327 concurrent sequences means 3,327 GDN state slots
   (18.63 MiB each = 62 GB) being read-modify-written every step. The fused GDN decode kernel is
   real, but its scaling at that slot count is unmeasured ⚠️.
2. **vLLM has no Blackwell CI at all** — its own RFC says CUDA CI covers only L4 and H100, and that
   FA4 and native NVFP4 paths are *"not yet qualified"* ([gpus/b300.md §11](../../gpus/b300.md)
   gotcha 7). FlashInfer, the default attention backend, is *"architecture-gated but not yet
   signoff-qualified"* on SM103a.
3. **The roofline ignores the vision tower entirely at decode** (correctly) **and the video decoder
   entirely at prefill** (incorrectly). torchcodec decode of 240 frames at 448×448 is unmeasured
   ([architecture.md §12](architecture.md) #6) and the vLLM captioning work exists precisely because
   CPU video decode bottlenecks before 4 GPUs.
4. **`--max-num-seqs` in practice.** No engine is configured for 3,327 concurrent sequences by
   default; the §3.2 KV-max rows are a capacity ceiling, not a recommended setting.

### 4.6 Comparison to the vendor API price, and break-even

[architecture.md §11.1](architecture.md) establishes that **there is no hosted Marlin-2B API**:
`inferenceProviderMapping` is `{}`, the base model has one text-only provider, and the author
monetises through a services motion, not a price list. **There is therefore no vendor $/1M anchor to
break even against, and no break-even utilisation can be computed.** ⚠️ **TO BE VERIFIED** — whether
Fireworks, Together, Replicate, Baseten, DeepInfra or SiliconFlow host it.

What can be said instead:

- **The absolute numbers make the comparison moot.** One B300 at `low` sustains ~15,200 two-minute
  captions/hour. A fleet captioning **1 million** 2-minute videos/month needs **~66 GPU-hours/month
  = $487** at `low`, $987 at `high`. The engineering time to stand up the `--hf-overrides` path and
  validate quality (§6) costs more than a year of that GPU bill.
- **Break-even against a hypothetical API** would be: an API priced at $X per 1M output tokens is
  beaten by self-hosting above `utilisation = $0.0278 / X` at `low` — i.e. any API charging more
  than **$0.028/1M output** loses to a fully-utilised B300, and more than **$0.28/1M** loses even at
  10 % utilisation. Since every general-purpose VLM API in
  [serving-optimizations.md §1.5](../../cross-cutting/serving-optimizations.md) charges $0.40–$50 per
  1M output, self-hosting wins on price at any plausible utilisation — **and that is exactly why the
  interesting question is not cost.**
- **The honest conclusion: B300 is the wrong GPU for this pair on cost grounds, and the cost is too
  small to care.** [architecture.md §11.2](architecture.md) puts `b200` at **$0.40/1k videos** and
  `h100` at **$0.46** against B300's **$0.49** — B300 loses to both, because it charges a capacity
  premium this 4.4 GB model cannot spend and an FP4 premium it has no checkpoint to use. The
  difference is **$0.09 per thousand videos**.

## 5. Scaling and deployment shape

### 5.1 Single node vs multi-node

**Single GPU. Then replicate.** There is no parallelism decision to make: 4.426 GB of weights, no
experts, no fabric requirement. An 8-GPU HGX B300 node runs **8 independent TP1 replicas**
(6,024 concurrent 2-minute videos node-wide, §1.3), front-ended by any HTTP load balancer. Scaling
past one node is linear and needs no InfiniBand.

**When PD disaggregation pays: it does not, here.** The case for it on B300 rests on the 1.6 TB/s of
per-node NIC bandwidth ([gpus/b300.md §4](../../gpus/b300.md)), and it pays when prefill and decode
have wildly different optimal parallelisms on a large MoE. For Marlin the entire prefill is
**186 ms** and the KV to migrate is **275.6 MiB** per request — the transfer is a meaningful
fraction of the work it would offload. **Do not disaggregate P from D.**

**When wide-EP pays: never.** Dense model, no experts.

**The one disaggregation worth investigating is EPD** — splitting the *encoder* out. The ViT is
**39 % of prefill compute** at 240 frames ([architecture.md §6.4](architecture.md)), it is
embarrassingly parallel across frames, and it is the phase competing with the video decoder for the
same request. TRT-LLM lists *EPD Disaggregated Serving = Yes* for `Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration`
([inference-engines.md §7.3](../../cross-cutting/inference-engines.md)) — but TRT-LLM support for
the Qwen3.5 *hybrid* path is itself ⚠️ unverified ([architecture.md §8.4](architecture.md)), so this
is a research direction, not a recipe.

### 5.2 Launch commands

**vLLM** — the architecture remap is the load-bearing flag, and `--hf-overrides` is documented API
for exactly this ([supported-models](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/models/supported_models.html)):

```shell
vllm serve NemoStation/Marlin-2B --port 8000 \
  --tensor-parallel-size 1 \
  --hf-overrides '{"architectures": ["Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration"]}' \
  --max-model-len 32768 \
  --mamba-cache-mode=align \
  --block-size 128 \
  --media-io-kwargs '{"video": {"num_frames": -1}}' \
  --mm-processor-cache-type shm
```

Quoted from [architecture.md §8.1](architecture.md) (which sources each flag), plus two B300-specific
additions justified here:

- **`--block-size 128`** — the FA4 hd256 kernel requires the KV block size to be a multiple of 128;
  *"larger blocks are split into 128-token kernel pages"*, and it falls back otherwise
  [src](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vllm-project/vllm/main/vllm/v1/attention/backends/flash_attn.py),
  restated as a not-optional flag in [flash-attention.md §17](../../cross-cutting/flash-attention.md).
  On B300 this is the single flag that turns the GPU's best Marlin-specific feature on.
- **`--mamba-cache-mode=align`** is **mandatory**, not an optimization: vLLM raises
  `NotImplementedError("Qwen3.5 currently does not support 'all' prefix caching, please use
  '--mamba-cache-mode=align' instead")`
  [src](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vllm-project/vllm/main/vllm/model_executor/models/qwen3_5.py).

For hardware video decode (NVDEC), add — flags verbatim from
[src](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-09-18-pynvvideocodec):

```shell
  --media-io-kwargs '{"video": {"backend": "pynvvideocodec"}}' \
  --mm-ipc-gpu-memory-gb 2
```

and run the container with `NVIDIA_DRIVER_CAPABILITIES=video,compute,utility`
[src](https://docs.nvidia.com/dynamo/multimodal/video-decode-gpu-requirements).

If CUDA-graph capture asserts in `causal_conv1d_update`, lower `--max-cudagraph-capture-size` below
the Mamba cache size (default 512) [src](https://docs.vllm.ai/projects/recipes/en/stable/Qwen/Qwen3.5.html).

**Container:** no Marlin-specific image exists. The published B300 images are model-specific
(`vllm/vllm-openai:deepseekv41-flash-0909`, `lmsysorg/sglang:dev-cu13-dsv41`) and irrelevant here;
use a stock vLLM ≥ v0.29.0 image on **CUDA ≥ 13** — required for the FlashInfer GDN prefill kernel
(§2). **Verify before deploying:** `cuobjdump --list-elf <lib>.so | grep sm_103`; an `sm_100a`-only
wheel will not load on B300 ([gpus/b300.md §11](../../gpus/b300.md) gotcha 2).

**transformers** (the only first-class path) — from [`MODEL_CARD.md`](MODEL_CARD.md) via
[architecture.md §8.6](architecture.md):

```python
marlin = AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained(
    "NemoStation/Marlin-2B", trust_remote_code=True,
    dtype=torch.bfloat16, device_map={"": "cuda"})
marlin.compile()

```

with the Path-A environment the model was trained under (`VIDEO_MAX_PIXELS=200704`, `FPS=2.0`,
`FPS_MAX_FRAMES=240`, `FPS_MIN_FRAMES=4`, `FORCE_QWENVL_VIDEO_READER=torchcodec`).

**SGLang** — same architecture remap; ⚠️ video ingestion for `qwen3_5` is unconfirmed
([architecture.md §8.4](architecture.md)), so validate before choosing it over vLLM on this GPU.

cold-start time for this stack on `sm_103`; budget minutes, not the second the weights take.

## 6. Risks and open questions

### Blocking — resolve before committing

1. **⚠️ Does the checkpoint actually load under `--hf-overrides` on vLLM?** All 618 tensor names must
   map onto `Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration`, and vLLM must handle the materialised `lm_head.weight`
   against `tie_word_embeddings: true` ([architecture.md §12](architecture.md) #2). Evidence is
   strong (pure subclass, unmodified forward, a third party already ships the remap) but **not
   verified end to end**. *New supporting evidence found for this document:* vLLM issue #36275 shows
   the failure mode for Qwen3.5 text-only checkpoints is a **weight-name mismatch** — vLLM expects
   `language_model.model.layers.*` while text-only checkpoints carry `model.layers.*`
   [src](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/36275). Marlin's weight map carries
   `model.language_model.embed_tokens.weight` ([architecture.md §3.2](architecture.md)), i.e. the
   **multimodal** naming — Marlin is on the *right* side of that bug. Encouraging, still not proof.
2. **⚠️ This repo contradicts itself on support status.**
   [`inference-engines.md`](../../cross-cutting/inference-engines.md) §6.5 states *"Engine support:
   none, on any engine"* and §8's master matrix says *"No engine. `transformers` +
   `trust_remote_code=True` only."* [architecture.md §8.1](architecture.md), written after gate
   access, concludes *"'vLLM-compatible' is true of the weights and false of the checkpoint as
   shipped. It costs one flag, not a port."* Both cite live sources; the engines doc predates the
   gate and did not consider `--hf-overrides`. **Treat the architecture doc as current and the
   engines doc §6.5 as stale**, but do not delete the caution: neither document has *run* it.
3. **⚠️ Path A vs Path B video token budget (23,520 vs 12,288), unresolved.** `qwen-vl-utils` (what
   `.caption()` uses) and the HF `Qwen3VLVideoProcessor` (what vLLM/SGLang use) differ by **1.91×**
   ([architecture.md §6.3](architecture.md)). **Every throughput and cost number in this document
   assumes Path A.** If your vLLM deployment silently takes Path B, throughput roughly doubles and
   quality silently drops. This is the single largest source of error in §§3–4.
4. **⚠️ Does serving through vLLM preserve caption and timestamp quality?** Fixed training prompt,
   `<think>` prefix artefact, a **two-element** EOS list `[248044, 248046]` that an engine reading
   only `config.eos_token_id` will half-miss, and a specific preprocessing path — four places to
   diverge, zero published engine-vs-transformers comparisons ([architecture.md §1.3, §12](architecture.md) #3).

### B300-specific engine risks

5. **⚠️ The INT4 path is broken by a live vLLM GDN bug.** vLLM issue #35924: Qwen3.5's GDN
   `in_proj_ba` uses `MergedColumnParallelLinear` with output dim = `num_v_heads`; when that drops
   below **`GPTQ_MARLIN_MIN_THREAD_N = 64`** the Marlin kernel raises a `ValueError` during weight
   loading. The issue's own table shows **Qwen3.5-27B failing at TP1 with 48 heads**
   [src](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/35924). **Marlin-2B has `num_v_heads` = 16**
   ([architecture.md §2.2](architecture.md)) — below the threshold **even at TP1**. So
   `prasannaJagadesh/marlin-2B-GPTQ-4BITS` is at risk on any vLLM build predating the fix
   (PR #36329, closed). BF16 is unaffected (it does not use the Marlin GEMM). ⚠️ Not verified
   against this specific checkpoint — test before choosing INT4 on B300. *(Note the name collision:
   the failing kernel is "Marlin", the W4A16 GEMM, not this model.)*
6. **⚠️ FlashInfer is "architecture-gated but not yet signoff-qualified" on SM103a** and is the
   *default* attention backend for vLLM and SGLang on Blackwell
   [src](https://docs.flashinfer.ai/api/attention.html). Default + unqualified. Regression-test.
7. **⚠️ vLLM has no Blackwell CI lane at all** (L4 and H100 only); FA4 and native NVFP4 are
   *"not yet qualified"* ([gpus/b300.md §11](../../gpus/b300.md) gotcha 7). Pin versions.
8. **⚠️ `sm_100a`-only wheels do not load on B300.** The #1 cause of "works on B200, dies on B300".
   Check with `cuobjdump --list-elf`.
9. **⚠️ The FA4 hd256 kernel falls back silently** if `--block-size` is not a multiple of 128, or if
   `mm_prefix` bidirectional attention, R-SWA, softcap, sinks or `decode_context_parallel_size > 1`
   are in play. **`mm_prefix` is a live concern for a VLM** ([architecture.md §8.3](architecture.md)):
   `supports_mm_prefix()` returns `is_fa_version_supported(4)`. If the model's video prefix uses
   bidirectional attention, B300's signature kernel may never engage. **Verify by profiling, not by
   reading flags.**
10. **⚠️ FlashInfer GDN prefill requires CUDA ≥ 13.** On a CUDA 12.x image, 18 of 24 layers silently
    drop to Triton/FLA. Nothing errors; throughput just falls.
11. **⚠️ B300's NVDEC configuration is not published.** NVIDIA's Dynamo table lists Blackwell
    **B200/GB200 at 7 NVDEC engines** with AV1 added, but **has no B300 row**
    [src](https://docs.nvidia.com/dynamo/multimodal/video-decode-gpu-requirements). For a *video*
    model on a GPU whose vendor cut INT8 by 24× and FP64 by 30× to buy FP4 throughput, it is worth
    confirming on silicon that the media engines survived intact before building a captioning fleet
    on them. Method: `nvidia-smi -q | grep -i decoder` on a real B300, and time a 240-frame NVDEC
    decode.

### Accuracy caveats of the executed precision

12. **BF16 is the executed precision, and it is the checkpoint's native dtype — so there is no
    quantisation accuracy risk at all on the recommended path.** That is the good news and it is
    unique to this pair in the roster.
13. **⚠️ Every quantised Marlin variant is unevaluated.** Not one of GPTQ-4bit, SDNQ-INT8, MLX-8bit
    or GGUF publishes a CaReBench / DREAM-1K / TimeLens score or a perplexity delta
    ([architecture.md §9](architecture.md)). For a model whose entire value is *second-precise
    timestamps*, 4-bit numeric error landing on the timestamp digits is a specific, plausible and
    **completely unmeasured** failure mode.
14. **⚠️ FP8 KV cache accuracy is unmeasured for this model.** It is the single best capacity lever
    on B300 (753 → 1,417 concurrent videos) and the one most likely to perturb exactly the output
    the model is bought for. Measure mIoU on TimeLens-Bench before enabling it in production.
15. **⚠️ NVFP4 KV at `head_dim 256` is unverified.** FlashInfer exposes `nvfp4_kv_cache_full_dim`;
    nobody has confirmed hd256.

### Measurement gaps that no amount of reading closes

16. **⚠️ No measured throughput or latency for Marlin-2B on any hardware, anywhere.** §3.2–§3.3 are
    roofline `est.`; §3.5 gives the proxy-derived haircut to apply.
17. **⚠️ No B300 MBU or MFU published by any engine** ([gpus/b300.md §9](../../gpus/b300.md)). MBU
    0.60 / MFU 0.40 are METHODOLOGY band midpoints.
18. **⚠️ No published FA4 or attention TFLOPS on `sm_103`** — and the "2× attention" claim, now
    quantified by NVIDIA as **5 → 10.7 TeraExponentials/s**
    [src](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/inside-nvidia-blackwell-ultra-the-chip-powering-the-ai-factory-era/),
    still has **no end-to-end measurement**. At 13.3 % attention share it is nearly irrelevant here
    regardless.
19. **⚠️ torchcodec / NVDEC decode throughput for 240 frames at 448×448 is unmeasured**, and the
    vLLM captioning work strongly suggests video I/O — not the GPU — is the production bottleneck
    for this exact workload ([architecture.md §12](architecture.md) #6).
20. **⚠️ GDN state-slot scaling at 753–3,327 concurrent sequences is unmeasured.** That is 14–62 GB
    of recurrent state read-modify-written every decode step; the fused kernel's behaviour there is
    the biggest unknown in §3.2's high-batch rows.
21. **⚠️ GDN conv-state width and dtype are engine-set.** `kernel` vs `kernel − 1` is 18.84 vs
    18.63 MiB/seq (+1.1 %, immaterial); `--mamba-ssm-cache-dtype bfloat16`, if honoured, halves the
    recurrent state to **9.63 MiB/seq** and would raise §1.2's concurrency by ~2 % at 23,520 ctx and
    ~30 % at 4K ctx. Worth testing ([architecture.md §5.2](architecture.md)).
22. **⚠️ Peak ViT activation footprint at 240 frames** under chunked prefill (§1.4).
23. **⚠️ InferenceX `compare/` pages are unaudited in this repo** and the Qwen3.5-B300 page returned
    two mutually inconsistent readings in this pass (§3.4 row 6). Do not cite it.
24. **⚠️ No hosted Marlin-2B API exists**, so METHODOLOGY §6's vendor sanity check cannot be
    performed and no break-even utilisation can be computed (§4.6).

### The strategic risk, stated plainly

25. **B300 is the wrong GPU for this model and nothing in this document changes that.** Its three
    differentiators over B200 are **+48.9 % HBM** (Marlin needs 4.4 GB of 268), **1.5× dense FP4**
    (no FP4 checkpoint exists, and the NVFP4 ridge point of 1,687 FLOP/byte is unreachable at this
    model's arithmetic intensity), and **2× attention SFU** (attention is 13.3 % of prefill FLOPs).
    Its BF16 rate and its bandwidth are **identical to B200**
    ([quantization-formats.md §4.3](../../cross-cutting/quantization-formats.md)), and B200 is
    cheaper (`planning price · b200 · low` **$6.00** vs `· b300 · low` **$7.40**). The one genuine
    B300-and-B200-only win for this model — the **FA4 hd256 kernel** — B200 has too. Run Marlin-2B
    on B300 only because the node is already there for something else; never buy one for it.
