## 0. Verdict

1. **Not runnable as shipped, on any engine, on any GPU — including this one.** `vLLM 0.29.0`
   and `SGLang 0.5.20` both register `Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration` and neither registers
   `MarlinForConditionalGeneration` ([inference-engines.md §6.5](../../cross-cutting/inference-engines.md)).
   The fix is one documented flag, **`--hf-overrides '{"architectures":["Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration"]}'`**
   ([architecture.md §8.1](./architecture.md)), and the weights are a pure subclass with an
   unmodified forward — but **nobody has run that remap on ROCm**, and vLLM's own Qwen3.5-2B
   recipe is validated on **Intel Arc Pro B60/B70 only, with no AMD cell at all**
   [src](https://recipes.vllm.ai/Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B). Engine: vLLM-ROCm ≥ 0.27.0 in
   `rocm/vllm:rocm10.0.0_ubuntu24.04_py3.14_pytorch_2.12.0_vllm_0.27.0`, or SGLang-ROCm 0.5.15+.
2. **1 GPU minimum, 1 GPU recommended, TP1, scale by data-parallel replicas (8 per UBB 2.0 node).**
   Weights are 4.426 GB against 259.2 GB usable — 1.7 % of the card. **TP beyond 2 is actively
   harmful**: there are only 2 KV heads, so TP4 duplicates each on 2 ranks and TP8 on 4 (§1).
3. **The weight format actually executed is BF16** — 2,500 TFLOPS dense, the *slowest* of the
   three native gfx950 matrix paths. No FP8, MXFP4, NVFP4 or AWQ checkpoint of Marlin-2B
   exists ([architecture.md §9](./architecture.md)); the one published 4-bit build is GPTQ-INT4,
   and **Marlin and Machete are CUDA-only, so INT4 on gfx950 is a Triton dequant that is
   *slower* than BF16** ([gpus/mi355x.md §6, §11](../../gpus/mi355x.md)). MI355X's headline
   10.1 PF MXFP4 rate is unreachable for this pair today.
4. **Interactive (S1, 4K in / 512 out, TPOT ≤ 50 ms): $0.066 per 1M output tokens; max
   throughput (S4): $0.058.** Both at **$8.60/GPU-hr**, which is simultaneously the `low` and
   the `high` tier because OCI `BM.GPU.MI355X.8` is the only published rate anywhere
   ([cloud-pricing.md §5.14](../../cross-cutting/cloud-pricing.md)). Blended (75 % in, half of
   it cached / 25 % out) **$0.024 per 1M tokens**. In the units that matter for this model:
   **$0.00116 per 2-minute video** at `.caption()`'s 2,048-token default, i.e.
   **$0.00058 per video-minute** — or **$1.16 per 1,000 captions**.
5. **Confidence: `speculative`.** Not because the arithmetic is weak — the fit is exact and the
   roofline is calibrated to MBU 0.50 against AMD's own measured Qwen3.5-27B run on a single
   MI355X (§3.2) — but because **the entire software path is unverified**: no engine has loaded
   this checkpoint on ROCm, video ingestion through `qwen3_5` on ROCm is unconfirmed, and the
   Gated-DeltaNet prefill path on gfx950 is the slowest tier in the stack with an open ROCm bug
   against this exact architecture family (§6). §1 alone is `estimate`-grade; §3 and §4 are not.

## 1. Fit

Fit is a hardware question and is answered here regardless of the engine verdict in §0.

**Pinned inputs.** METHODOLOGY §8: MI355X **288 GB** HBM3E as deployed, 8.0 TB/s, dense
BF16 2,500 / FP8 5,000 / MXFP4 & MXFP6 10,100 TFLOPS. `usable = 0.90 × 288e9 = 259.2 GB`
(= 241.40 GiB, [gpus/mi355x.md §2](../../gpus/mi355x.md)). Model: 2,213,241,664 unique params,
**4.426 GB BF16 resident** (tie honoured) / 5.444 GB on disk, **12,288 B/token KV** across 6
of 24 layers, **19,537,920 B (18.63 MiB) fixed GDN state per sequence**, `S = 1`
([architecture.md §3–§5](./architecture.md)). Activation workspace **4 GB**, the middle of
METHODOLOGY §3's 2–6 GB band.

> **One disagreement to record, not to paper over.** ROCm's GPU-specifications table heads its
> capacity column "VRAM (**GiB**)" with the value 288, which would be 309.2 GB
> [src](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/en/latest/reference/gpu-arch-specs.html); AMD's product page,
> the CDNA 4 architecture page and the acceptance guide all say **288 GB**, and METHODOLOGY §8
> pins 288 GB. This document uses 288e9 bytes throughout
> ([gpus/mi355x.md §2](../../gpus/mi355x.md)).

> **A second, model-side one.** `model.safetensors.index.json` ships `lm_head.weight`
> materialised *and* `tie_word_embeddings: true`. A loader that honours the tie keeps
> **4.426 GB**; one that does not keeps **5.444 GB**. At 259.2 GB usable the 1.02 GB delta is
> 0.4 % of the card and changes no decision — but the **decode weight read is 3.764 GB either
> way**, because `lm_head` is read once per step regardless of whether a second copy sits idle
> in HBM. Whether vLLM's `Qwen3_5` loader honours it is ⚠️ **TO BE VERIFIED**
> ([architecture.md §8.1](./architecture.md), open question 2).

### 1.1 Fit over the GPU counts this topology allows

MI355X's scale-up domain is a **fully-meshed 8-GPU UBB 2.0 baseboard with no switch and no
NVL72 equivalent** ([gpus/mi355x.md §4](../../gpus/mi355x.md)), so the allowed set is
{1, 2, 4, 8} intra-node and {16, 32, …} only across 400G RoCE/IB. GB300's 18/36/72 rows do not
apply to this GPU.

```
### Fit over GPU counts allowed by the MI355X topology (UBB 2.0, scale-up max 8)

| GPUs | parallelism | fabric | weights/GPU (GB) | of which replicated vision+norms | act. ws (GB) | KV budget/GPU (GB) | KV B/tok aggregate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | TP1 (no sharding) | single GPU | 4.426 | 0.664 | 4 | 250.77 | 12,288 |
| 2 | TP2 | intra-node Infinity Fabric mesh | 2.545 | 0.664 | 4 | 252.65 | 12,288 |
| 4 | TP4 — KV heads replicated | intra-node Infinity Fabric mesh | 1.605 | 0.664 | 4 | 253.60 | 24,576 |
| 8 | TP8 — KV heads replicated | intra-node Infinity Fabric mesh | 1.134 | 0.664 | 4 | 254.07 | 49,152 |
| 16 | TP16 — KV heads replicated | MULTI-NODE (2 nodes) over 400G RoCE/IB | 0.899 | 0.664 | 4 | 254.30 | 98,304 |
| 32 | TP32 — KV heads replicated | MULTI-NODE (4 nodes) over 400G RoCE/IB | 0.782 | 0.664 | 4 | 254.42 | 196,608 |
```

`est.` `weights_per_gpu(N) = ((1,881,825,088 − 548,672)/N + 548,672 + 331,416,576) × 2 B`;
`kv_budget = 259.2e9 − weights_per_gpu − 4e9`.

**Multi-node fabric needed? No, never, for any reason.** A single GPU holds the model plus
2,086 concurrent 8 K sequences or 811 concurrent 2-minute videos. The 16- and 32-GPU rows are
printed only to show that they are arithmetically legal and strategically pointless: they cross
400G Ethernet/IB for a model that fits 58× over on one card, and METHODOLOGY §4's 20–40 %
multi-node TP comm penalty would apply to every decode step.

### 1.2 Max concurrency by context

```
### Max concurrency by context — BF16 KV / FP8 KV  (S = 1 GDN state slot)

| GPUs | 8K | 32K | 128K | 1M | 23,560 (2-min video) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2,086 / 3,589 | 593 / 1,135 | 153 / 304 | 19 / 38 | 811 / 1,526 |
| 2 | 4,203 / 7,232 | 1,196 / 2,287 | 309 / 612 | 39 / 78 | 1,635 / 3,075 |
| 4 | 4,592 / 8,439 | 1,229 / 2,402 | 313 / 622 | 39 / 78 | 1,694 / 3,282 |
| 8 | 4,814 / 9,202 | 1,246 / 2,464 | 314 / 627 | 39 / 78 | 1,726 / 3,395 |
| 16 | 4,932 / 9,637 | 1,255 / 2,495 | 315 / 629 | 39 / 78 | 1,742 / 3,455 |
```

`est.` `max_concurrency(ctx, N) = floor( N × kv_budget / (ctx × 12,288 × max(1, N/2) + 19,537,920) )`.
The 1-GPU row reproduces [architecture.md §8.5](./architecture.md)'s MI355X row exactly
(811 at 23,520; 593 at 32 K; 153 at 128 K).

⚠️ **The 1 M column is arithmetic only.** `max_position_embeddings` is **262,144** and
`rope_type` is `default` with no YaRN/NTK entry ([architecture.md §5.3](./architecture.md)), so
1 M is out of spec for this checkpoint. At the real native ceiling of 262,144 tokens the numbers
are **77 (BF16 KV) / 153 (FP8 KV)** concurrent sequences on one GPU.

⚠️ **The 128 K column is unreachable with video.** The 240-frame cap bounds every video request
at ~23,560 prefill tokens no matter how long the clip is
([architecture.md §6.3](./architecture.md)). 32 K/128 K/262 K are text-prompt-only rows.

### 1.3 TP is the wrong lever here, and the table above says why

```
### Concurrency per GPU spent — TP vs replicas (BF16 KV, 8K ctx)

| shape | total GPUs | max concurrency | per GPU |
|---|---|---|---|
| TP1 | 1 | 2,086 | 2,086 |
| TP2 | 2 | 4,203 | 2,102 |
| TP4 | 4 | 4,592 | 1,148 |
| TP8 | 8 | 4,814 | 602 |
| 2 x TP1 replicas | 2 | 4,172 | 2,086 |
| 4 x TP1 replicas | 4 | 8,344 | 2,086 |
| 8 x TP1 replicas | 8 | 16,688 | 2,086 |
```

**TP4 and TP8 buy almost nothing over TP2, and TP2 buys nothing over two independent replicas.**
The cause is `num_key_value_heads: 2`. Sharding KV heads is exact to TP2; beyond that each head
is duplicated, so the *aggregate* KV bytes per token double at TP4 and quadruple at TP8, exactly
cancelling the extra HBM. TP8 delivers **602 concurrent sequences per GPU against TP1's 2,086 —
a 3.5× loss** — while adding an all-reduce to every decode step over the 1,075 GB/s mesh
(METHODOLOGY §4's 5–15 % TP8 comm band). **TP1 × 8 replicas per node is the only correct shape.**

### 1.4 What cannot be sharded

| Tensor group | Params | Shardable? | Consequence on MI355X |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Language 2-D linear weights (MLP, GDN projections, attention proj) | 1,372,717,056 | ✅ column/row TP; GDN by head (16 K / 16 V heads → clean to TP16) | fine at any TP in scope |
| **Full-attention `k_proj` / `v_proj` — only 2 KV heads** | 12,582,912 | ❌ **beyond TP2** | at TP4 each KV head lives on 2 ranks, at TP8 on 4; **aggregate KV storage multiplies by `max(1, N/2)`** (§1.3) |
| `embed_tokens` / `lm_head` (248,320 × 2,048, one stored copy each) | 508,559,360 | ✅ vocab-parallel | — |
| `linear_attn.conv1d` (depthwise), `A_log`, `dt_bias`, all RMSNorms, `q_norm`/`k_norm`, `linear_attn.norm`, `model.norm` | **548,672** | ❌ **replicated on every rank** | 1.1 MB/rank — negligible, but it is the floor `weights_per_gpu(N)` converges to |
| **Entire vision tower (24-block ViT + merger)** | **331,416,576** | ❌ **replicated** under `--mm-encoder-tp-mode data` | **0.663 GB on every rank.** Data-parallel ViT is the consistent recommendation across every multimodal model in this repo ([serving-optimizations.md §6.2](../../cross-cutting/serving-optimizations.md)) and is what vLLM's MI355X Qwen3-VL command sets [src](https://recipes.vllm.ai/Qwen/Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B-Instruct) |
| **GDN recurrent + conv state** (18.63 MiB/seq) | — | ✅ by head (16 V heads) | aggregate state per sequence is invariant to TP ≤ 16 |
| MTP head | **0 — not shipped** | — | `mtp_num_hidden_layers: 1` is declared in `config.json` but **zero `mtp` tensors exist** in the 618-name manifest ([architecture.md §2.3, §7.1](./architecture.md)). Fixes METHODOLOGY §2's `S` at **1** |

### 1.5 Where the bytes go in a decode step

```
  S1 b=1: weights 3.76 GB (98%) | KV 0.06 GB (1%) | GDN state r+w 0.04 GB (1%) | total 3.86 GB
  S1 b=256: weights 3.76 GB (13%) | KV 14.50 GB (51%) | GDN state r+w 10.00 GB (35%) | total 28.26 GB
  S1 b=2008: weights 3.76 GB (2%) | KV 113.70 GB (58%) | GDN state r+w 78.46 GB (40%) | total 195.93 GB
  SV b=128: weights 3.76 GB (8%) | KV 40.28 GB (82%) | GDN state r+w 5.00 GB (10%) | total 49.04 GB
  SV b=750 (KV ceiling): weights 3.76 GB (1%) | KV 236.00 GB (88%) | GDN state r+w 29.31 GB (11%) | total 269.07 GB
```

`est.` `bytes = 3.7637e9 + batch × ctx × 12,288 + batch × 2 × 19,537,920` (read **and** write of
the live GDN slot; the `S−1` prefix checkpoints are a memory cost, not a bandwidth one).

Two crossovers fall out, both computed in `python3`:

- **At the video context (25,608 tokens) KV read overtakes weight read at batch 12.0**; at the
  S1 context (4,608) not until batch 66.5. ([architecture.md §6.2](./architecture.md) puts the
  video crossover at 13.0 for its 23,520-token prefill-only context; 12.0 here because this
  document carries the 2,048 output tokens into `ctx`.)
- **Below 1,590 tokens of context the fixed GDN state costs more than the KV cache**
  ([architecture.md §5.2](./architecture.md)) — irrelevant for video, decisive for a short-text
  fleet.

The second block row is the one worth staring at: **at short context and high batch, the GDN
recurrent state read-modify-write is 35–40 % of all bytes moved per decode step.** That is a
Marlin-specific fact — 18 of 24 layers each read and write a 1,048,576-element fp32 state per
sequence per step — and it is the term that caps this model's high-concurrency throughput long
before the weights do.

## 3. Throughput and latency

### 3.1 Assumptions, stated per METHODOLOGY §4

```
bytes_per_decode_step(batch) = 3.7637e9 + batch × ctx × kv_B_per_token + batch × 2 × 19,537,920
decode_step_time ≈ max( bytes / (8.0e12 × MBU),  2 × 1,881,825,088 × batch / (2500e12 × MFU) )
prefill_time(T)  ≈ [ 2 × 1,881,825,088 × T / (2500e12 × MFU)
                    + 24,576 × T² / (2500e12 × MFU_attn) ] × GDN_penalty
                    + frames × 0.2722e12 / (2500e12 × MFU)        ← ViT, video only
```

| Parameter | Value used | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **MBU** | **0.50** | METHODOLOGY §4 gives **0.4–0.6 for MI355X/ROCm**; [architecture.md §10.3](./architecture.md) pins 0.50 for the `mi355x` row. **Calibrated** — see §3.2 |
| **MFU (dense BF16 GEMM)** | **0.30** | [architecture.md §10.3](./architecture.md)'s `mi355x` row; the bottom of METHODOLOGY's 0.35–0.5 band, per [gpus/mi355x.md §9](../../gpus/mi355x.md)'s *"plan at the bottom of each band until you have a measurement"* |
| **MFU_attn** | **0.18** | **not a band value — a measurement.** CK FA2 forward, bf16, **hd256**, b=8/s=5121/18 heads = **436 TFLOPS** ÷ 2,500 TF = 17.4 % [src](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/5403). ⚠️ that run is on **MI350X**, so 0.18 is a cross-part ratio; it is still the only hd256 attention measurement AMD has published |
| **GDN prefill penalty** | **×1.37** on the LLM prefill term, `est.` | the inverse of aiter#5606's measured *"median TTFT reduced by 27 %"* when the 4-kernel Triton GDN prefill chain is replaced by one fused gfx950 Gluon kernel [src](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/5606). ⚠️ measured end-to-end on Qwen3-Next-80B through SGLang, **not** on a 2 B model; applied to the whole LLM prefill because the FLOP roofline cannot see kernel-launch cost (GDN is 0.5 % of prefill FLOPs but 75 % of the layers) |

### 3.2 The measured anchor, and what it does and does not license

**There is no published throughput or latency measurement of Marlin-2B on any hardware**
([architecture.md §10.2](./architecture.md), re-confirmed this pass: the card has no
Performance section, HF `model-index` is `null`, it is not an MLPerf model, and
InferenceX/InferenceMAX carries no Marlin rows). There is likewise **no MI355X measurement of
any 2 B-class hybrid-GDN model**, and **vLLM's own Qwen3.5-2B recipe carries no AMD hardware
cell** [src](https://recipes.vllm.ai/Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B).

What does exist is AMD's own single-MI355X benchmark of **Qwen3.5-27B** — the same
`qwen3_5` architecture, the same hybrid GDN schedule, the same `head_dim: 256` GQA, on the
same GPU [src](https://rocm.blogs.amd.com/artificial-intelligence/dflash-on-MI355x/README.html).
Environment: AMD Instinct MI355X, **single GPU, gfx950, TP=1**; vLLM `0.22.1rc1.dev43+g8c3cc98cf`
(ROCm build); **ROCm 7.2.3**; PyTorch 2.10.0; AITER 0.1.13 (bf16) / ≥ 0.1.16.post2 (mxfp4);
128 prompts, max output 4,096, concurrency 1 and 32.

[qwen3827b/mi355x.md §3.2](../qwen3827b/mi355x.md) back-solves that run to an implied
**MBU of 0.47–0.55** on this GPU for this architecture family, and finds its own roofline at
MBU 0.50 lands within −9 % / +7 % of the measurement. **That is the licence for MBU 0.50 here,
and it is the only calibration this document has.**

**What it does not license, stated plainly.** Qwen3.5-27B reads 55.6 GB of weights per decode
step, so its step is ~99 % weight stream and the byte roofline is nearly exact. Marlin-2B reads
**3.76 GB** — a step 15× smaller — so at low batch the fixed per-step costs the roofline cannot
see (kernel launch, Triton dispatch across 18 GDN layers, sampling, detokenisation, the
Python-side scheduler) are a **majority** of the step, not a rounding error. **The `+1.0 ms`
column in §3.3 is not decoration; at batch 1 it doubles TPOT.** Treat the roofline column as an
upper bound that a 27 B model would nearly reach and a 2 B model will not.

### 3.4 The scenario this model actually serves

METHODOLOGY's S1–S3 are text scenarios. Marlin-2B's real operating point is fixed by the
240-frame cap: **every video request of ≥ 2 minutes is exactly ~23,560 prefill tokens**, and
`.caption()` defaults to `max_new_tokens=2048` ([architecture.md §6.3, §6.5](./architecture.md)).
Call it **SV: 23,560 in / 2,048 out, ctx 25,608**.

`est.` **+70 % output throughput at the video operating point for one flag.** At video context
KV is 82 % of the bytes moved (§1.5), so halving it is nearly a straight halving of the step.
This is the largest available optimisation for this pair on this GPU, and it is free.

unit of concurrency is queueing delay, scheduler load and TTFT risk for nothing. Batch 256 is
the operating point; 2,008 and 3,292 are printed so the arithmetic is auditable, not as
recommendations.

**Explaining the gap between `est.` and `meas.` — there is no gap to explain, because there is
no measurement.** What can be said about the *direction* of the error:

1. **The estimates are optimistic on prefill by an unquantified further amount.** The ×1.37 GDN
   penalty is derived from an 80 B MoE where GDN is a smaller share of the work; on Marlin the
   GDN layers are 18 of 24 and the model is 15× smaller, so kernel-launch overhead per layer is
   a *larger* fraction. vllm#51406's finding that ROCm also falls back to a "slow multi-op eager
   path" for the fused QK-norm+RoPE+gate kernel is a second, unmodelled penalty on the same
   layers. **Both push the same way.**
2. **The estimates are optimistic on decode at low batch by ~1 ms/step**, which is the `+floor`
   column — at batch 1 that is a **2× error**, at batch 256 a 14 % one.
3. **The estimates are optimistic at very high batch** for reasons no roofline models: a vLLM
   scheduler managing 2,000–3,000 concurrent sequences with per-sequence GDN state bookkeeping is
   itself a CPU bottleneck. This is why §3.5 recommends batch 256.
4. **They are pessimistic in exactly one place**: MFU_attn 0.18 comes from a CK measurement on
   the *air-cooled MI350X*, which runs a flat ~8.3 % below MI355X on every matrix rate
   ([gpus/mi355x.md §3](../../gpus/mi355x.md)). Correcting for the part would raise MFU_attn to
   ~0.19 — worth ~2 % of TTFT at the video context. It does not offset (1)–(3).

**Net: treat §3.3–§3.5 as an upper bound.** [architecture.md §11.2](./architecture.md) reaches
the same verdict from the other direction, flagging MI355X as *"the one row whose throughput
carries an unquantified downward correction"* and calling its own $0.68/1k-videos figure *"an
upper bound on what it would actually deliver."* This document quantifies part of that
correction and lands at **$1.16/1k videos** (§4.4).

### 4.2 Cost per 1M tokens

`cost_per_1M = $/GPU-hr ÷ (tok/s/GPU × 3600) × 1e6`. Input cost uses the scenario's prefill rate
at 0 % cache hit. Blended is METHODOLOGY §6 verbatim — **75 % input (50 % of it cached at ~10 %
of uncached cost) / 25 % output** — i.e. `0.75 × 0.55 × cost_in + 0.25 × cost_out`.

```
### Cost per 1M tokens — 1 GPU, prices from cloud-pricing.md §5.14 (planning price · mi355x)

| operating point | out tok/s/GPU | $/1M output @ $8.60 (low=high=res1y) | $/1M output @ $1.42 colo (est.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 interactive (conc 256) | 36,232 | $0.066 | $0.011 |
| S1 at the SLO ceiling (conc 2,008) | 40,995 | $0.058 | $0.010 |
| S4 max throughput (conc 3,292) | 41,304 | $0.058 | $0.010 |
| SV video fleet, conc 128 | 10,440 | $0.229 | $0.038 |

| input stream | in tok/s/GPU | $/1M input @ $8.60 | $/1M input @ $1.42 (est.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4K text prompt | 139,249 | $0.017 | $0.003 |
| 32K text prompt | 107,220 | $0.022 | $0.004 |
| 2-min video (ViT+LLM) | 81,074 | $0.029 | $0.005 |

Blended $/1M tokens (75% in, half of it cached at 10% cost / 25% out) at the S1 operating point:
  $8.60 low=high: $0.0236 per 1M blended tokens
  $1.42 colo est.: $0.0039 per 1M blended tokens
S4 blended: $0.0215 @ $8.60, $0.0036 @ $1.42
```

**S1 and S4 differ by 12 % on output cost and 9 % blended.** On a 288 GB card serving a 2 B
model, capacity is so far from binding that the interactive and batch operating points nearly
coincide — the same structural situation
[qwen3827b/mi355x.md §3.4](../qwen3827b/mi355x.md) reports for the 27 B sibling, only more
extreme.

### 4.3 What speculative decoding and prefix caching would do to these numbers

| Lever | Effect on cost here | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Speculative decoding** | **Zero, today — no drafter exists.** If one did, AMD's own same-family same-GPU measurement suggests the ceiling: DFlash block=16 gave **5.02×** single-request throughput on Qwen3.5-27B, which at batch 1 would take $/1M output from $2.30 to $0.46. **But the gain collapses at batch.** METHODOLOGY §5 and [serving-optimizations.md §2.5](../../cross-cutting/serving-optimizations.md) both note speculation stops paying at high batch, and this fleet runs at batch 128–256. At the S1 operating point the realistic gain is **single-digit percent, not 5×** | [src](https://rocm.blogs.amd.com/artificial-intelligence/dflash-on-MI355x/README.html) |
| **Prefix caching** | **≈ Zero on the video workload.** Every request carries a different video; the cacheable span is a 30–40-token scaffold, **< 0.2 % of a 23,560-token request**. The blended formula above still credits 50 % of input as cached at 10 % cost because METHODOLOGY §6 defines it that way — **for a real Marlin video fleet, recompute blended at 0 % hit: `0.75 × cost_in + 0.25 × cost_out` = $0.0294/1M @ $8.60 instead of $0.0236, i.e. the METHODOLOGY blended figure understates this workload's cost by 25 %** | [architecture.md §5.5](./architecture.md) |
| Prefix caching, the one case it works | A batch job asking several *different questions about the same video* — which is what `--mamba-cache-mode=align` exists for. **`.multi_find` is not that case**: it re-trims the video with ffmpeg, so each pass has a different prefix, and costs `N × (re-encode + full prefill + decode)` | [architecture.md §1.4, §5.5](./architecture.md) |
| **FP8 KV cache** | **The lever that actually pays on decode.** +69.7 % output tok/s at the video operating point (§3.4) takes SV $/1M output from **$0.229 → $0.135**. On the *fleet* rate the gain is much smaller, because prefill is the limiter and FP8 KV does nothing for it: decode-bound 18,351 → 31,137 videos/h but sustained only **7,396 → 8,862**, i.e. **$1.163 → $0.970 per 1k videos** (`est.`) | §3.4, §4.4 |
| **Quantising the weights yourself (Quark FP8 or MXFP4)** | **The second lever, and nobody has pulled it.** Prefill is the sustained limiter (§4.4), and FP8 doubles the dense rate 2,500 → 5,000 TFLOPS while MXFP4 quadruples it to 10,100. `est.` at METHODOLOGY's FP8 MFU 0.25 (linear term at 5,000 TFLOPS/0.25, attention term unchanged at 2,500 TFLOPS/`MFU_attn` 0.18 since attention stays BF16, ViT unchanged, same ×1.37 GDN penalty as §3.1 — recomputed 2026-09-19, see Audit log): prefill-bound 12,388 → **15,942** videos/h, sustained 7,396 → **8,531**, **$1.163 → $1.008 per 1k videos** — and combined with FP8 KV the two would compound. ⚠️ **No such checkpoint exists, and no accuracy evaluation of any quantised Marlin variant has ever been published** ([architecture.md §9](./architecture.md)) | [gpus/mi355x.md §3, §9](../../gpus/mi355x.md) |

### 4.4 Video economics — the units that matter for this model

Sustained fleet rate per METHODOLOGY §6, combining a compute-bound prefill and a
bandwidth-bound decode on one GPU: `sustained = 1/(1/prefill_bound + 1/decode_bound)`.
Frames per request follow the model's own policy, `clamp(2 fps × duration, 4, 240)`, at
200,704 px/frame → 98 LLM tokens/frame ([architecture.md §6.3](./architecture.md)).

```
### Video economics — sustained fleet, 240-frame (>=2 min) requests

| 768 out (demo Space default) | conc 128 | prefill-bound 12,388 vid/h | decode-bound 51,031 vid/h | sustained 9,968 vid/h/GPU | @$8.60: $0.863/1k videos, $0.000863/video, $0.000431/video-minute |
| 2048 out (.caption default)  | conc 128 | prefill-bound 12,388 vid/h | decode-bound 18,351 vid/h | sustained 7,396 vid/h/GPU | @$8.60: $1.163/1k videos, $0.001163/video, $0.000581/video-minute |
| 768 out  | @$1.42 colo est. | | | | $0.142/1k videos, $0.000142/video, $0.000071/video-minute |
| 2048 out | @$1.42 colo est. | | | | $0.192/1k videos, $0.000192/video, $0.000096/video-minute |
```

**Three readings.**

1. **Every row is prefill-bound**, the opposite of the batch-1 picture. `12,388` videos/h from
   prefill against `18,351–51,031` from decode: **prefill FLOPs are the thing to optimise**,
   which is exactly why §4.3's FP8/MXFP4 row is the biggest untaken lever, and why the missing
   fused GDN prefill kernel (aiter#5606) costs real money.
2. **Cost per video-minute is a trap past 2 minutes.** The 240-frame cap means a 2-minute and a
   10-minute clip cost **the same per request**, so $/video-minute falls from **$0.00058** (2 min)
   to **$0.000116** (10 min) — while effective sampling drops from 2.0 fps to 0.40 fps. The
   cheaper number buys a worse caption. **Price per request, not per minute**, and chunk long
   video client-side ([architecture.md §6.3](./architecture.md)).
3. **This is not a cost-constrained workload.** A fleet captioning 1 M two-minute videos per
   month at `.caption()`'s full 2,048 tokens needs **135 GPU-hours ≈ $1,163/month on one
   MI355X** at OCI's rate — `est.`, and that is the *most expensive* published rate for this
   silicon. Engineering time dominates the bill by an order of magnitude, and §0's software risk
   is worth far more than §4's price spread.

**Batch-1 single-request latency**, for completeness:

```
Batch-1 single 2-min video: ViT 87 ms + LLM prefill 204 ms + decode 2048 x 1.03 ms = 2.40 s
  (+ 2.05 s if the 1.0 ms/step ROCm floor holds -> 4.45 s)
```

`est.` — **85 % of that is decoding 2,048 caption tokens**, so the batch-1 lever is output
length (`.find()` caps at 64; the author's own demo Space uses 768), not the GPU.
[architecture.md §6.5](./architecture.md) puts the same figure at 2.08 s for decode alone on a
pure roofline; the difference is this document's per-step overhead term.

## 5. Scaling and deployment shape

### 5.1 The shape

**One GPU, TP1, one vLLM-ROCm process per GPU, eight independent replicas per UBB 2.0 node,
a load balancer in front, and nothing else.** Concretely:

- **Single node vs multi-node: single, always.** §1.1 — the model fits 58× over on one card, and
  MI355X's 8-GPU scale-up ceiling ([gpus/mi355x.md §4](../../gpus/mi355x.md)) is irrelevant to a
  shape that never leaves one GPU.
- **When does PD disaggregation pay for this pair? Never.** Dynamo has no documented ROCm
  support, and PD is *"a thousand-GPU problem"*
  ([serving-optimizations.md §4.3](../../cross-cutting/serving-optimizations.md)).
- **When does wide-EP pay? Never — there are no experts.** This is the one pair in the repo where
  MI355X's missing NVL72 equivalent, its standard structural weakness, costs nothing at all.
- **The one disaggregation worth thinking about is EPD**, splitting the ViT off from prefill and
  decode: the vision tower is 39 % of prefill compute and is a small, pure-GEMM, high-MFU
  workload that a cheaper card could run. ⚠️ No ROCm evidence exists
  ([serving-optimizations.md §6.3](../../cross-cutting/serving-optimizations.md)).
- **What actually needs scaling is video decode, not the model.** 240 frames at 448×448 per
  request, and the ROCm story for that is `rocDecode`
  [src](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/rocDecode/en/latest/how-to/using-rocDecode-video-decoder.html)
  with **no published fps figure and no documented torchcodec integration**. The author's own
  reference deployment sets `FORCE_QWENVL_VIDEO_READER=torchcodec`
  ([architecture.md §8.6](./architecture.md)). Budget this as the real capacity-planning unknown.

### 5.2 Launch command

⚠️ **There is no published Marlin-2B recipe for any GPU, and no published Qwen3.5-2B recipe for
any AMD GPU** ([src](https://recipes.vllm.ai/Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B) lists Intel Arc Pro B60/B70 only).
The command below is **assembled**, not quoted from a single source; every flag is attributed.

```bash
# Container: ROCm 10.0.0 / vLLM 0.27.0 — the tag AMD's docs publish
#   [src] https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/ai-ecosystem/en/latest/inference/vllm.html
docker pull rocm/vllm:rocm10.0.0_ubuntu24.04_py3.14_pytorch_2.12.0_vllm_0.27.0

# --- AITER + KV layout. Required, not optional, in every published AMD recipe.
#   [src] https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-02-27-rocm-attention-backend
export VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER=1
export VLLM_ROCM_SHUFFLE_KV_CACHE_LAYOUT=1
export SAFETENSORS_FAST_GPU=1
#   [src] https://recipes.vllm.ai/Qwen/Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B-Instruct  (vLLM's own MI355X VLM command)
export VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER_MHA=1
export HIP_FORCE_DEV_KERNARG=1

# --- The Path A video preprocessing the model was trained with.
#   [src] models/marlin2b/architecture.md §6.3, §8.6 (the author's Space sets these verbatim)
export FORCE_QWENVL_VIDEO_READER=torchcodec
export VIDEO_MAX_PIXELS=200704
export FPS=2.0
export FPS_MAX_FRAMES=240
export FPS_MIN_FRAMES=4

vllm serve NemoStation/Marlin-2B \
  --hf-overrides '{"architectures": ["Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration"]}' \
  --tensor-parallel-size 1 \
  --max-model-len 32768 \
  --mamba-cache-mode=align \
  --attention-backend ROCM_AITER_FA \
  --kv-cache-dtype fp8_e4m3 \
  --mm-encoder-tp-mode data \
  --media-io-kwargs '{"video": {"num_frames": -1}}' \
  --gpu-memory-utilization 0.90 \
  --max-num-batched-tokens 32768 \
  --max-num-seqs 256 \
  --enable-chunked-prefill
```

Flag-by-flag provenance:

| Flag | Why, and where from |
|---|---|
| `--hf-overrides '{"architectures": …}'` | `MarlinForConditionalGeneration` is unregistered in vLLM and SGLang; `--hf-overrides` is documented vLLM API for exactly this remap, and a third party already ships the remapped checkpoint ([architecture.md §8.1](./architecture.md); [src](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/models/supported_models.html)) |
| `--tensor-parallel-size 1` | §1.3 — TP > 2 duplicates the 2 KV heads |
| `--mamba-cache-mode=align` | vLLM **raises `NotImplementedError`** for Qwen3.5 with `mamba_cache_mode == "all"` [src](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vllm-project/vllm/main/vllm/model_executor/models/qwen3_5.py) |
| `--attention-backend ROCM_AITER_FA` | the recommended ROCm MHA/GQA backend; supports head size 256 and `fp8_e4m3` KV [src](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/design/attention_backends/), 3.61× over `ROCM_ATTN` on MI355X [src](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-02-27-rocm-attention-backend) |
| `--kv-cache-dtype fp8_e4m3` | +69.7 % output tok/s at the video operating point (§3.4); set by AMD's own MI355X recipes [src](https://recipes.vllm.ai/zai-org/GLM-5.2) |
| `--mm-encoder-tp-mode data` | in vLLM's published MI355X VLM command [src](https://recipes.vllm.ai/Qwen/Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B-Instruct); a no-op at TP1, correct the moment TP > 1 |
| `--media-io-kwargs '{"video":{"num_frames":-1}}'` | needed to stop the HF video processor from silently halving the frames (the Path A vs Path B 1.91× mismatch, [architecture.md §6.3](./architecture.md)) ⚠️ |
| `--max-num-batched-tokens 32768` | **MI355X-specific:** InferenceMAX found the default chunked-prefill size of 196,608 *"produced crash dumps; reducing to 32,768 resolved it"* ([gpus/mi355x.md §11](../../gpus/mi355x.md)) |
| `--max-num-seqs 256` | §3.5 — throughput saturates here; higher only adds queueing |

**SGLang alternative** (`lmsysorg/sglang` built from the repo's `rocm.Dockerfile`, ROCm path is
gfx942/gfx950-only [src](https://docs.sglang.io/platforms/amd_gpu.html)):

```bash
export SGLANG_USE_AITER=1
python -m sglang.launch_server --model-path <marlin-2b-with-remapped-architectures> \
  --port 8000 --tp-size 1 --mem-fraction-static 0.8 --context-length 32768 \
  --attention-backend triton --linear-attn-backend triton --kv-cache-dtype fp8_e4m3
```

`--attention-backend triton` because SGLang's own guidance for the full-attention layers of a
hybrid GDN model is *"AMD (ROCm): `triton` recommended"*, and `--linear-attn-backend triton`
because the CuTe-DSL and FlashInfer GDN backends are CUDA-only
[src](https://docs.sglang.io/advanced_features/attention_backend.html). ⚠️ SGLang video
ingestion for `qwen3_5` was **not confirmed** ([architecture.md §8.4](./architecture.md), open
question 15), and SGLang's AITER path applies a **0.85 multiplier to `--mem-fraction-static`
above 8 K context**, so `0.8` lands at ≈0.68 and every §1.2 concurrency figure drops ~24 %
([qwen3827b/mi355x.md §1.4](../qwen3827b/mi355x.md)) ⚠️ unverified for this model.

### 5.3 Warm-up and load time

```
### Weight load from NVMe
  5 GB/s: 1.09 s for the 5.444 GB checkpoint (0.89 s for the 4.426 GB resident set)
  10 GB/s: 0.54 s for the 5.444 GB checkpoint (0.44 s for the 4.426 GB resident set)
  20 GB/s: 0.27 s for the 5.444 GB checkpoint (0.22 s for the 4.426 GB resident set)
```

`est.` **Weight loading is a rounding error — one second at worst.** `SAFETENSORS_FAST_GPU=1` is
set in AMD's recipes and makes it faster still. What actually dominates cold start on this
platform:

- **Triton JIT / autotune for the 18 GDN layers.** ROCm has no precompiled GDN kernel; the
  Triton path compiles on first use. ⚠️ No published warm-up figure for gfx950 GDN kernels
  exists — budget **minutes, not seconds**, on a cold container, and warm the server with
  synthetic requests before taking traffic.
- **HIP-graph capture.** ⚠️ Watch for the `causal_conv1d_update` assertion when the graph capture
  size exceeds the mamba cache size — lower `--max-cudagraph-capture-size`
  [src](https://docs.vllm.ai/projects/recipes/en/latest/Qwen/Qwen3.5.html).
- **No NVFP4 requantisation step.** The 10–55 s online NVFP4→MXFP4 load penalty
  ([gpus/mi355x.md §6](../../gpus/mi355x.md)) does not apply: the checkpoint is BF16.

## 6. Risks and open questions

### Blocking — resolve before committing this pair

1. ⚠️ **Does `--hf-overrides` actually load this checkpoint on vLLM-ROCm?** The evidence is
   strong (pure subclass with an unmodified forward, confirmed in the gated `modeling_marlin.py`;
   a third party already ships the remap; `--hf-overrides` is documented API) but **nobody has
   run it on any GPU, let alone on gfx950**. Sub-question: how vLLM handles the materialised
   `lm_head.weight` against `tie_word_embeddings: true`
   ([architecture.md §8.1](./architecture.md), open question 2).
2. ⚠️ **Does video ingestion work for `qwen3_5` on ROCm at all?** vLLM's supported-models table
   lists `Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration` with video modality, and vLLM's ROCm verification for
   Qwen3.5 covers the **397 B MoE text** path — **not** a 2 B hybrid **video** path
   ([architecture.md §8.5](./architecture.md); [gpus/mi355x.md §2](../../gpus/mi355x.md)).
   Video + GDN + ROCm is unverified in combination. This is open question 10 of
   [architecture.md §12](./architecture.md), and it is this document's single largest risk.
3. ⚠️ **The Path A vs Path B video token budget, 23,520 vs 12,288 tokens (1.91×).** Serving
   through vLLM's HF video processor likely halves frames or resolution silently — a quality
   regression, not an error. Every capacity and cost number in this document assumes **Path A**
   ([architecture.md §6.3](./architecture.md)). If your deployment lands on Path B, halve the
   prefill cost and accept an unmeasured quality loss.
4. ⚠️ **GatedDeltaNet + dp-attention is a known crash on MI355X.** `HIP error: invalid
   configuration argument` faulting in `chunk_gated_delta_rule_fwd_*` on 8×MI355X / ROCm 7.2
   with Qwen3.5-397B; **closed with no workaround but "disable dp-attention"**
   [src](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/issues/31594). The recommended shape here is TP1
   × independent replicas, which sidesteps it — but it is direct evidence that the GDN Triton
   path on gfx950 is not settled.
5. ⚠️ **No accuracy evaluation of Marlin-2B exists under *any* precision on *any* hardware**, and
   none of the quantised community variants reports a CaReBench / DREAM-1K / TimeLens score, a
   perplexity delta, or anything ([architecture.md §9](./architecture.md)). **BF16 is executed
   here, so there is no precision risk from this document's recommendation** — but the moment
   anyone quantises to FP8/MXFP4 to chase §4.3's prefill win, they are the first person to do so,
   and for a model whose value is *second-precise timestamps*, numeric error landing on the
   timestamp digits is a specific, plausible, entirely unmeasured failure mode.

### High-impact but non-blocking

6. ⚠️ **No measurement of this pair, or of any 2 B hybrid-GDN model on MI355X, exists.** Every
   number in §3 and §4 is `est.` The nearest anchor is a 27 B sibling (§3.2).
7. ⚠️ **The ROCm GDN prefill penalty is estimated, not measured for this model.** ×1.37 is
   aiter#5606's `−27 % TTFT` inverted, measured on an 80 B MoE through SGLang. On an 18-of-24-layer
   2 B model the true penalty is plausibly larger. Two open PRs would remove most of it
   ([aiter#5606](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/5606),
   [vllm#51406](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/51406)) — **watch both.**
8. ⚠️ **The +1.0 ms per-step ROCm overhead floor is an estimate** back-derived from one ATOM
   measurement on a different model. At batch 1 it doubles TPOT; at batch 256 it is 14 %. It is
   shown as a separate column precisely because it is the least-defensible number here.
9. ⚠️ **rocDecode throughput for 240 frames at 448×448 is unpublished, and no torchcodec-on-ROCm
   integration is documented.** For a video model this is plausibly the real production
   bottleneck, and it is the least-mapped part of the pipeline on this platform
   ([architecture.md §10.3](./architecture.md), open question 6, is the same worry on CUDA).
10. ⚠️ **MFU_attn = 0.18 rests on a single CK FA2 measurement taken on MI350X, not MI355X**
    ([flash-attention.md §15.3](../../cross-cutting/flash-attention.md) flags this correction
    explicitly). No published attention TFLOPS figure for gfx950 at any head dim exists
    ([gpus/mi355x.md §5](../../gpus/mi355x.md)).
11. ⚠️ **`--kv-cache-dtype fp8_e4m3` accuracy on this model is unmeasured.** The general evidence
    is good (+14.9 % throughput, "at most 1–2 points" degradation,
    [quantization-formats.md §8.2](../../cross-cutting/quantization-formats.md)), but no one has
    checked what FP8 KV does to timestamp precision on a temporal-grounding model.
12. ⚠️ **SGLang's 0.85 `--mem-fraction-static` multiplier above 8 K context under AITER** is
    documented on the Qwen3.8 (2.4 T) page only; whether it applies to a dense 2 B GDN model is
    unverified ([qwen3827b/mi355x.md §1.4](../qwen3827b/mi355x.md)). If it does, every §1.2
    concurrency figure drops ~17–24 % on the SGLang path.
13. ⚠️ **Whether HIP-graph full capture succeeds for a hybrid-GDN video model on gfx950** is
    unverified; the known `causal_conv1d_update` assertion is documented for Qwen3.5 generally.
14. ⚠️ **`--mamba-ssm-cache-dtype bfloat16`**, if honoured, would halve the GDN state to
    9.63 MiB/seq and cut the state traffic that is 35–40 % of a high-batch decode step (§1.5).
    Unverified for this model on any platform ([architecture.md §5.2](./architecture.md)).
15. ⚠️ **The MI355X price rests on one seller.** `low` = `high` = `res1y` = $8.60 (OCI). No
    reserved tier is published, AWS/GCP/Azure carry no SKU, and every neocloud routes to sales.
    Every figure in §4 scales linearly with a number that has no second source
    ([cloud-pricing.md §5.13, §5.14](../../cross-cutting/cloud-pricing.md)).
16. ⚠️ **`.multi_find` cost in production is unmeasured.** N sequential ffmpeg `libx264`
    re-encodes plus N full prefills; the CPU video work is invisible to every roofline in this
    document ([architecture.md §1.4](./architecture.md)).

### Accuracy caveats of the executed precision

**BF16, unquantised, all 618 tensors — there are none.** This is the reference precision the
model was trained and released in, and [quantization-formats.md §9.6](../../cross-cutting/quantization-formats.md)
marks it *"native"* on every GPU in the roster with the footnote *"Format support is total and
uninteresting for a BF16 checkpoint."* The precision risk on this pair is exactly zero, and that
is the one unambiguously good thing about running Marlin-2B on MI355X: **you pay in throughput
(2,500 TFLOPS instead of 10,100) and you buy back perfect numerical fidelity to the release.**

The accuracy caveats that *do* apply are one level up and are not precision at all: the
240-frame cap silently sampling a 10-minute clip at 0.40 fps, the Path A/B preprocessing
mismatch, the `<think>` prefix that raw `generate()` callers must strip, and the two-element EOS
list (`[248044, 248046]`) that an engine reading only `config.eos_token_id` will get wrong
([architecture.md §1.3, §6.3](./architecture.md)).
