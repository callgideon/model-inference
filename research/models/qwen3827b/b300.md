## 0. Verdict

1. **Runnable today, but nobody has run it.** vLLM 0.29.0 publishes a B300 launch profile
   (`hw/b300.json`, TP1, image `vllm/vllm-openai:qwen38`) and lists `b300` in the
   `supported_hardware` of its `fp8`, `nvfp4` and `int4` variants — but **B300 is not in the
   `meta.hardware` verified set** (`gb300`, `rtx_5090`, `rtx_5090_2x`, `rtx_pro_6000`,
   `dgx_spark_gb10`, `ascend_950pr` are) [src](https://recipes.vllm.ai/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B.json), and
   **SGLang 0.5.20 has no B300 cell for this model at all** — its verified list is `h200`,
   `rtx6000`, `rtx5090`, `dgx-spark`, `gb300` ([inference-engines.md §6.4](../../cross-cutting/inference-engines.md)).
   GB300 — the same `sm_103` silicon in a higher clock bin — **is** verified on both engines, so
   this is a coverage gap, not a capability gap. **⚠️ TO BE VERIFIED end-to-end on HGX B300.**
2. **Min 1 GPU, recommended 1 GPU.** Every precision fits one B300 with room to spare
   (NVFP4 21.92 GB against a 241.2 GB usable budget). The right node shape is **TP1 × 8
   independent replicas per node (data parallel)**, not TP8 — TP only buys KV budget, and
   past TP4 it stops buying even that because the model has only 4 KV heads (§1).
3. **Weight format actually executed: mixed NVFP4 + FP8 + BF16**, natively, on `sm_103` —
   NVFP4 W4A4 on the 64 MLPs and `lm_head`, FP8 on the 16 self-attention and 48
   linear-attention projections, BF16 on the vision tower, all norms and the MTP head
   ([quantization-formats.md §9.4, §9.6](../../cross-cutting/quantization-formats.md)). **This
   caps the B300 FP4 story: only 71.7 % of GEMM FLOPs reach the 13,500 TFLOPS FP4 pipes; the
   other 28.2 % run FP8, where B300 is identical to B200** (§3).
4. **Interactive (S1, TPOT ≤ 50 ms), bs 256, TP1: $0.165–0.334 per 1M output tokens**
   (blended $0.060–0.122 per 1M). **Max throughput (S4), bs 384: $0.153–0.309 per 1M output.**
   Prices are `b300` `low` $7.40/GPU-hr (Hyperstack) and `high` $15.00/GPU-hr
   (OCI `BM.GPU.B300.8`), [cloud-pricing.md §5.14](../../cross-cutting/cloud-pricing.md).
   The TPOT SLO never binds on this pair — **KV capacity (396 concurrent 4.5 K sequences)
   and prefill compute do**.
5. **Confidence: `estimate`.** Every throughput and cost figure here is roofline `est.` at
   MBU 0.60; zero measurements of *this model* on *this GPU* exist anywhere
   ([architecture.md §10.1](./architecture.md)). The nearest real measurements are
   SemiAnalysis InferenceX rows for **Qwen-3.5-397B-A17B — the same `qwen3_5` hybrid
   Gated-DeltaNet + gated-GQA architecture family — on `b300`** (§3.2). Derate the estimates
   ~30 % per [architecture.md §10.2](./architecture.md)'s one calibration point.

> **Use bf16 GDN state on B300 — it is not only a memory choice.** SGLang auto-selects the
> **FlashInfer GDN prefill fast path** on SM100/SM103 with CUDA 13+ only when *"the base
> linear-attention backend is Triton, recurrent state is **BF16**, key/value head dimensions
> are 128, dynamic chunking and page-major KV layout are disabled, and `--chunked-prefill-size`
> is between 1 and 8192"* ([architecture.md §8.4](./architecture.md),
> [flash-attention.md §14.3](../../cross-cutting/flash-attention.md)). This model's
> `linear_key_head_dim` / `linear_value_head_dim` are both 128 ✅. Every table below therefore
> uses the **bf16** state; the fp32 figures are 1.96× larger per slot.

### 1.2 What cannot be sharded

| Tensor group | Shardable? | Consequence on B300 |
|---|---|---|
| **The 4 GQA KV heads** (`k_proj`, `v_proj`, and the KV cache itself) | **Only to TP4.** `num_key_value_heads: 4` [architecture.md §2.2](./architecture.md) | At TP8 vLLM/SGLang give each rank 1 KV head and **replicate each head across 2 ranks** — node-wide KV bytes double, so TP8 holds *no more sequences than TP4 at the same context*. At TP16 the factor is 4. This is the single most important shape fact in the table below. |
| **All RMSNorm weights** — 65 LM norms, 16 × (`q_norm`,`k_norm`), 48 GDN `norm`, `A_log`, `dt_bias`, MTP norms | No (replicated) | ~679 K params ≈ 1.36 MB BF16 per rank. Negligible. |
| **Vision tower** (0.921 GB BF16, never quantised by any published recipe — [architecture.md §3.2](./architecture.md)) | Sharded under TP, **replicated under `--mm-encoder-tp-mode data`** | vLLM's own B300 command sets `--mm-encoder-tp-mode data` [src](https://recipes.vllm.ai/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B/hw/b300.json), so the tower is **replicated on every rank**: +0.921 GB/GPU at any TP. The weights/GPU column below includes this. |
| **The `S − 1` GDN checkpoint buffers** | No — they are per *request*, not per rank | 4 × 78.4 MB = 313.8 MB per running request at SGLang's default, on top of the live slot. This is what makes short-context concurrency state-bound (§1.4). |
| **GDN recurrent state (live slot)** | Yes, by head (48 value / 16 key heads → clean to TP16) | Aggregate state per sequence is TP-invariant; only its per-rank share shrinks. |
| **MLPs, `embed_tokens`, `lm_head`, GDN projections, attention Q/O** | Yes (column/row/vocab-parallel) | Clean to TP16. |

**Multi-node fabric: never needed.** A 27.78 B dense model at any precision is a single-GPU
model on a 268 GB card. TP16 is printed below only because the topology allows it; it costs
20–40 % comm overhead over InfiniBand (METHODOLOGY §4) to buy concurrency you can get for
free by running 8 TP1 replicas.

✅ The TP1 FP8-KV column reproduces [architecture.md §10.2](./architecture.md)'s B300 row
(325 / 620 at 8 K, 146 / 186 at 32 K, 45 / 49 at 128 K, 6 / 6 at 1 M) exactly.

**Read the diminishing returns.** TP1 → TP4 multiplies 8 K FP8-KV concurrency by 4.3×
(325 → 1,398). TP4 → TP8 multiplies it by only 1.44×, and TP8 → TP16 by 1.27× — because the
4 KV heads replicate and because the per-GPU weight term is already negligible at TP4
(6.17 GB of a 241 GB budget). **Eight TP1 replicas give 8 × 325 = 2,600 concurrent 8 K
sequences per node against TP8's 2,011, at zero comm overhead.** TP exists here only to
raise the *single-sequence* ceiling: at 1 M context TP1 holds 6 sequences and TP8 holds 27.

### 1.4 Fit at TP1, all weight formats

| weight format | weights/GPU (GB) | KV+state budget (GB) | 8K FP8-KV (S=5/1) | 32K | 128K | 262K | 1M |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| BF16 55.56 | 55.56 | 181.6 | 274 / 523 | 123 / 157 | 38 / 41 | 20 / 20 | 5 / 5 |
| FP8 30.87 | 30.87 | 206.3 | 312 / 594 | 140 / 179 | 44 / 47 | 22 / 23 | 5 / 5 |
| **NVFP4 21.92** (`nvidia`, `RadixArk`) | 21.92 | 215.3 | **325 / 620** | **146 / 186** | **45 / 49** | 23 / 24 | **6 / 6** |
| NVFP4 26.38 (`Inferact` — what vLLM's `nvfp4` variant points at for `b300`) | 26.38 | 210.8 | 319 / 607 | 143 / 182 | 44 / 48 | 23 / 24 | 6 / 6 |
| INT4 W4A16 19.45 | 19.45 | 217.7 | 329 / 627 | 148 / 188 | 46 / 49 | 24 / 25 | 6 / 6 |

**Quantisation buys almost nothing in *fit* on this card.** BF16 → NVFP4 is a 33.64 GB
saving on a 241 GB budget: 8 K concurrency rises 274 → 325 (+19 %), 128 K rises 38 → 45
(+18 %). On B300 the case for NVFP4 is **compute and decode bandwidth**, not capacity — the
opposite of the case on a 32 GB RTX 5090 ([architecture.md §8.3](./architecture.md)).

**The `S` multiplier still halves short-context concurrency.** At 8 K FP8-KV, `S` = 5 gives
325 and `S` = 1 gives 620. At 128 K the two converge (45 vs 49) because the KV term
dominates. One GDN slot is worth 2,394 FP8-KV tokens; at `S` = 5 that is **11,970 tokens per
request** ([architecture.md §5.2](./architecture.md)) — so any B300 fleet serving agentic
turns shorter than ~12 K tokens is **state-bound, not KV-bound**, and
`--disable-radix-cache` nearly doubles its concurrency at the price of every prefix
re-prefill ([architecture.md §5.6](./architecture.md)).

**1 M context.** One B300 at NVFP4 + FP8 KV holds **6 concurrent 1 M sequences** (34.5 GB
each). At BF16 KV it holds 3. At BF16 *weights* with BF16 KV it holds 2. 1 M on this model
means NVFP4 weights, FP8 KV and a Blackwell-Ultra capacity class — B300 is one of only three
GPUs in this repo that clears 3 ([architecture.md §10.2](./architecture.md)).

## 3. Throughput and latency

### 3.1 Assumptions (METHODOLOGY §4)

```
MBU (decode)  = 0.60   central; METHODOLOGY §4 band for Blackwell first-gen software is 0.50-0.70
                       gpus/b300.md §9 marks B300-specific MBU/MFU "TO BE VERIFIED" - no engine publishes one
MFU (prefill) = 0.25 on the NVFP4 GEMMs  (METHODOLOGY §4 FP4 band 0.20-0.35)
                0.30 on the FP8  GEMMs   (METHODOLOGY §4 FP8 band 0.25-0.40)
                0.50 on attention        (FA4 reaches 0.71 of dense BF16 peak on B200 for pure attention,
                                          tridao.me/blog/2026/flash4 via gpus/b300.md §9; derated for hd256 under load)
comm overhead = 0 (TP1). 8 % / 12 % / 15 % at TP2 / TP4 / TP8 if you deviate.

Decode bytes per step - METHODOLOGY §4 EXTENDED per architecture.md §6.3, because the GDN state
must be read AND written back every step for every running sequence:

  bytes(batch, ctx) = weights + batch x ctx x kv_bytes_per_token + batch x 2 x state_bytes
                      (dense model: weights_read = all weights, batch-independent)
  The S multiplier is a MEMORY term, not a BANDWIDTH one: only the live slot is touched per step.

  ctx for decode bytes = in + out/2 (mean over the generation); ctx for feasibility = in + out.
```

**The prefill rate is a blend, and that is the headline for B300.** From
[architecture.md §3](./architecture.md)'s per-group parameter counts and
[quantization-formats.md §9.4](../../cross-cutting/quantization-formats.md)'s layer map:

```
GEMM FLOPs/prefill-token = 51.25 GFLOP
   NVFP4 (64 MLPs 17.113 B + lm_head 1.271 B)        = 36.77 GFLOP   71.7 %   @ 13,500 x 0.25 = 3,375 TFLOP/s
   FP8   (48 GDN proj 5.537 B + 16 attn qkvo 1.678 B)= 14.43 GFLOP   28.2 %   @  4,500 x 0.30 = 1,350 TFLOP/s
   BF16  (conv1d, in_proj_a/b, A_log, dt_bias)       =  0.05 GFLOP    0.1 %
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   effective blended GEMM rate = 51.25 / (36.77/3375 + 14.43/1350) = 2,365 TFLOP/s  est.
   pure-NVFP4 ceiling would be 3,375 TFLOP/s -> the FP8 28 % costs 50 % of the prefill time
attention rate (FA4-class) = 2,250 x 0.50 = 1,125 TFLOP/s  est.
attention FLOPs(T)         = 16 x 2 x 24 x 256 x T^2   (architecture.md §6.2)
```

> **B300's 1.5× FP4 uplift reaches only 71.7 % of this model's GEMM FLOPs, and that share is
> already the *fast* half.** Because the FP8 groups run at 1,350 vs the FP4 groups' 3,375
> TFLOP/s — a 2.5× gap, not 4× — they consume ~50 % of the prefill time while being 28 % of
> the work — and **FP8 is
> bit-for-bit identical on B200** ([gpus/b300.md §12(b)](../../gpus/b300.md),
> [quantization-formats.md §4.3](../../cross-cutting/quantization-formats.md)). A B300 will
> not be 1.5× a B200 on this checkpoint in prefill; the arithmetic ceiling is
> `1 / (0.5/1.5 + 0.5) = 1.20×`, before any memory or attention effect. Everything else
> B300 gives this model is **capacity** (268 vs 180 GB = +48.9 %) and the unmeasured 2×
> softmax/SFU claim.

**Three readings:**

1. **The TPOT SLO never binds.** The largest feasible S1 batch (396) has TPOT 29.3 ms,
   comfortably inside 50 ms. On B300 this model is limited by KV capacity and by prefill
   compute, never by decode latency. The `infeasible (KV)` cells at S2-256 and S3-64+ are
   the real ceiling.
2. **The decode-only column is a lie for 0 %-cache traffic.** At S1/bs 256, decode alone
   says 12,463 tok/s/GPU; charging the same GPU for the 92 ms prefill of every 4 K prompt
   drops the sustained rate to **3,856 tok/s at 0 % hit**, 5,940 at 50 %, and 10,245 at
   90 %. With only 512 output tokens per 4 K prompt, **this workload is prefill-bound above
   bs ≈ 40 unless you have prefix reuse.** `preserve_thinking: true` exists precisely to
   give you that reuse ([architecture.md §5.6](./architecture.md)).
3. **State traffic is 41 % of the decode step at the S1 operating point.** At bs 256 /
   4,352 ctx: 21.9 GB weights + 36.5 GB KV + **40.2 GB of pure GDN state read+write**. At
   fp32 state it would be 78.8 GB and the step would be 39 % slower. This is the strongest
   argument on B300 for `--mamba-ssm-dtype bfloat16` — which is also the flag that unlocks
   the FlashInfer GDN prefill kernel (§2).

### 3.4 Why the estimates and the measurements differ

- **The one calibration point available over-predicts by ~35 %.** Applying this roofline to
  an RTX 5090 at NVFP4 + MTP, bs = 1 predicts ~207 tok/s against SGLang's measured 152.9 —
  the shortfall is draft-model overhead and imperfect acceptance
  ([architecture.md §10.2](./architecture.md)). Hence the ×0.7 column in §3.2; treat every
  `est.` here as an optimistic ceiling.
- **The Qwen-3.5 proxy rows sit far below the estimates for structural reasons, not
  software ones.** They are a 397 B MoE on TP2–TP4 with EP all-to-all in the loop; this
  model is 27.78 B dense on TP1 with no collectives at all. Per-GPU weight traffic differs
  by an order of magnitude. Use the proxy for *ratios* (MTP decay, B300/B200, TPOT-vs-conc
  shape), never for absolute tok/s.
- **TTFT is the least trustworthy column.** METHODOLOGY §4 asks for it; computing it needs
  a GDN-prefill MFU that nobody publishes ([architecture.md §10.2](./architecture.md)). The
  proxy's `bf16 / mtp / TP4 / conc 64` row shows a **14.6 s median TTFT** — a scheduler
  pathology, not a compute figure, and a reminder that chunked-prefill tuning dominates
  real TTFT. SGLang's own `--chunked-prefill-size 2048` guidance for hybrid-GDN models
  exists because *"decode steps stall behind each prefill chunk … 8192-token chunks stall
  them ~600 ms at a time"* ([architecture.md §8.4](./architecture.md)).
- **An FA2-vs-FA4 fork sits inside the attention term.** §3.1 assumes an FA4-class
  1,125 TFLOP/s. If you pin `--kv-cache-dtype fp8` on vLLM you get FA2 instead (§2), and
  the 32.4 %-attention 128 K prefill in §3.2 gets materially slower. ⚠️ Nobody has measured
  either kernel on `sm_103`.

Using the *measured* B300 MTP speedups from §3.3 (same-image FP8/TP4 rows) applied to the

| bs | out tok/s/GPU | with MTP (measured proxy speedup) | $/1M out | with MTP |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 32 | 4,875 | 6,630 (×1.36) | $0.4216 | **$0.3100** |
| 64 | 7,476 | 9,719 (×1.30) | $0.2750 | **$0.2115** |
| 128 | 10,196 | 12,337 (×1.21) | $0.2016 | **$0.1666** |
| 256 | 12,463 | 14,333 (×1.15) | $0.1649 | **$0.1434** |

MTP is **free** — the 0.425 B head ships in the BF16, FP8 and INT8 checkpoints; both NVFP4
exports keep it BF16 (+0.85 GB) rather than dropping it
([architecture.md §7.1](./architecture.md)). It costs `D` = 4 state slots per request, which
on a 268 GB card is affordable. The first-principles crossover for NVFP4 weights
(`batch × (ctx × 32,768 + 2 × 78.45 MB) < weights_bytes`,
[architecture.md §7.4](./architecture.md)) puts the break-even at **bs ≈ 73 at S1, 18 at
S2, 5 at S3** — yet the measured proxy still shows 1.15× at concurrency 256, so the
first-principles bound is conservative. **Turn MTP on; turn it off only if you measure it
hurting.** ⚠️ No acceptance-vs-batch curve for *this* checkpoint exists.

### 4.2 Effect of prefix caching

Input cost scales with uncached tokens; at the `low` tier and S1's 4 K prompt:

| prefix-cache hit | effective prefill tok/s/GPU | $/1M input | blended $/1M (S1, bs 256) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 % | 44,671 | $0.0460 | $0.0757 |
| 50 % (METHODOLOGY §6 default) | 90,793 | $0.0226 | **$0.0602** |
| 90 % | 460,621 | $0.0045 | $0.0478 |

The **throughput** effect is larger than the cost effect: §3.2's sustained column moves
3,856 → 5,940 → 10,245 tok/s/GPU across the same three hit rates, i.e. a 90 %-hit workload
gets **2.7× the sustained output of a 0 %-hit one on identical hardware**. For agentic
traffic — OpenRouter reports a **92 % hit rate on Kimi K3 traffic**
([serving-optimizations.md §1.5](../../cross-cutting/serving-optimizations.md)) — the 90 %
column is the realistic one, and `preserve_thinking: true` is what keeps it there.

### 4.3 Versus the vendor API

Qwen Cloud list price ([architecture.md §11.1](./architecture.md)):
**$0.50 / 1M input, $3.00 / 1M output**, explicit cache read $0.05 (exactly 10 % of input,
matching METHODOLOGY §6's self-serving assumption). Under the same 75/25 blend with half
the input on explicit cache:

```
API blended = 0.75 x (0.5 x 0.50 + 0.5 x 0.05) + 0.25 x 3.00 = $0.9563 per 1M tokens
```

| tier | our blended $/1M (S1, bs 256) | **break-even utilisation vs API** |
|---|---:|---:|
| low $7.40 Hyperstack | $0.0602 | **6.3 %** |
| res1y $7.94 DigitalOcean | $0.0646 | **6.8 %** |
| high $15.00 OCI `BM.GPU.B300.8` | $0.1221 | **12.8 %** |

i.e. a single B300 running at **7 % of its S1 capacity** already matches Qwen's list price;
at the 0.7-derated rate it is ~9–18 %. On output alone the gross margin is **9× to 18×**
($0.165–0.334 self-hosted against $3.00). On *input* the gap is far narrower — $0.046–0.093
self-hosted against $0.50 list, ~5–11×, and against the cheapest OpenRouter fp4 provider
(Darkbloom, $0.100 in / $1.800 out) it is ~1–2× on input. **Self-hosting on B300 wins
decisively on output-heavy reasoning traffic — and `reasoning_effort: xhigh` is this
model's *default* ([architecture.md §1.4](./architecture.md)) — and only modestly on
input-heavy traffic.** The caveat is that only Novita and Alibaba serve the full 1 M window
([architecture.md §11.2](./architecture.md)); if you need 1 M, self-hosting is not a cost
decision.

**Cross-check against [architecture.md §11.3](./architecture.md).** That table puts B300
NVFP4 at $0.219/1M output at bs 256 and $0.255 at bs 128, using a flat 8 K context. This
document gets $0.165 at bs 256 because S1's mean context is 4,352, not 8,192 — recomputing
at ctx 8 K reproduces §11.3 exactly (98.6 → 130.8 GB/step, 20.54 → 27.25 ms, 12,463 → 9,394
tok/s, $0.1649 → $0.2188). Same method, different scenario; no disagreement.

## 5. Scaling and deployment shape

**Single node, and not even that.** The recommended shape on an HGX B300 is **eight
independent TP1 replicas, one per GPU**, behind a router. That gives 8 × 325 = **2,600
concurrent 8 K sequences per node** at NVFP4 + FP8 KV + `S` = 5, against TP8's 2,011, with
zero NVLink traffic and eight independent failure domains. TP exists only to raise the
single-sequence context ceiling (TP8 holds 27 concurrent 1 M sequences vs TP1's 6) or to cut
TPOT at very low concurrency. vLLM's own `compatible_strategies` for this model is
`["single_node_tp"]` — there is no published PP, DP-attention or PD layout
([architecture.md §8.6](./architecture.md)).

**Multi-node never pays.** A 21.92 GB model on a 268 GB card has nothing to disaggregate.
PD disaggregation is a thousand-GPU problem at this scale
([serving-optimizations.md §7.3](../../cross-cutting/serving-optimizations.md)), wide-EP is
meaningless (no experts), and the open question — whether the GDN recurrent state survives a
NIXL handover at all — is ⚠️ unanswered. The one thing B300's 1.6 TB/s of NIC bandwidth buys
this model is fast **weight staging and KV offload** (vLLM reports `offloading_cpu` and
`offloading_fs` both `verified` for this model; whether the *GDN state* offloads with it is
⚠️ — [architecture.md §8.6](./architecture.md)).

**Launch commands.**

vLLM's own B300 profile, quoted verbatim
[src](https://recipes.vllm.ai/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B/hw/b300.json) (image `vllm/vllm-openai:qwen38`,
strategy *"Single-node tensor parallel. Splits the model across all local GPUs."*):

```bash
VLLM_USE_RUST_FRONTEND=1 vllm serve Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B \
  --tensor-parallel-size 1 \
  --enable-auto-tool-choice --tool-call-parser qwen3_xml \
  --reasoning-parser qwen3 \
  --mm-encoder-tp-mode data
```

For an NVFP4 B300 deployment, vLLM's `nvfp4` variant — the one whose
`supported_hardware` contains `b300` — points at **`Inferact/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4`** (26.38 GB),
*not* `nvidia/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4`, whose `nvfp4_nvidia` variant declares only
`["rtx_pro_6000","dgx_spark_gb10"]` ([inference-engines.md §6.4](../../cross-cutting/inference-engines.md),
[src](https://recipes.vllm.ai/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B.json)). Quoted verbatim
[src](https://recipes.vllm.ai/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B):

```bash
vllm serve Inferact/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4 \
  --tensor-parallel-size 1 \
  --max-model-len 262144 \
  --kv-cache-dtype fp8 \
  --reasoning-parser qwen3 \
  --enable-auto-tool-choice --tool-call-parser qwen3_xml
```

> *"Add `--speculative-config '{"method":"mtp","num_speculative_tokens":3}'` for MTP."*

SGLang has no B300 cell; the **GB300 cell is the nearest published `sm_103` recipe**, quoted
verbatim with its template placeholders as published
[src](https://docs.sglang.io/cookbook/autoregressive/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B.md) — note it
deliberately omits `--attention-backend flashinfer` (which the SM120 cells carry), because
FlashInfer is not a legal full-attention backend for a hybrid-GDN model on SM100/SM103 (§2):

```
--trust-remote-code --model-path {{MODEL_NAME}} --kv-cache-dtype fp8_e4m3
--mem-fraction-static 0.85 --chunked-prefill-size 2048
--reasoning-parser qwen3 --tool-call-parser qwen3_coder --host {{HOST_IP}} --port {{PORT}}
```

On B300 add, for the reasons in §1.1 and §2:
`--mamba-ssm-dtype bfloat16` (unlocks the FlashInfer GDN prefill kernel and cuts 41 % of the
decode step's state traffic to 21 %) and an explicit `--attention-backend triton` or `fa4`
until [sglang#21904](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/issues/21904)'s SM103
`trtllm_mha` fix is confirmed on your build. ⚠️ Neither addition is in any published B300
recipe.

YaRN to 1 M, quoted verbatim from the model card
[src](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B/raw/main/README.md):

```shell
SGLANG_ALLOW_OVERWRITE_LONGER_CONTEXT_LEN=1 python -m sglang.launch_server ... --json-model-override-args '{"text_config": {"rope_parameters": {"mrope_interleaved": true, "mrope_section": [11, 11, 10], "rope_type": "yarn", "rope_theta": 10000000, "partial_rotary_factor": 0.25, "factor": 4.0, "original_max_position_embeddings": 262144}}}' --context-length 1000000
```

Qwen's caveat: *"All the notable open-source frameworks implement static YaRN … potentially
impacting performance on shorter texts."* At 1 M a single B300 holds 6 concurrent sequences
(§1.3), so this is a niche lane, not a default.

**Warm-up and loading.** Raw NVMe read time for the weights is trivial on a
`p6-b300.48xlarge` (8 × 3.84 TB local NVMe = 30.72 TB,
[gpus/b300.md §11.13](../../gpus/b300.md)):

| checkpoint | at 20 GB/s | at 5 GB/s |
|---|---:|---:|
| NVFP4 21.92 GB | 1.1 s | 4.4 s |
| FP8 30.87 GB | 1.5 s | 6.2 s |
| BF16 55.56 GB | 2.8 s | 11.1 s |

**Wall-clock to READY is dominated by everything else.** SGLang reports the BF16 checkpoint
takes **~6.5 minutes to load its 18 shards from NVMe on a DGX Spark** and advises budgeting
**~10 minutes to READY before calling a boot hung**
([architecture.md §1.1](./architecture.md)). A B300 host (192 vCPU, 4 TB RAM) should be far
faster at the safetensors parse, but add CUDA-graph capture across the batch ladder and
FlashInfer/CuTeDSL JIT warm-up — vLLM's GDN prefill backend emits a JIT warm-up warning
([flash-attention.md §14.3](../../cross-cutting/flash-attention.md)). **⚠️ TO BE VERIFIED:
no B300 cold-start measurement for this model exists.** Budget 3–10 minutes and measure.

## 6. Risks and open questions

**⚠️ TO BE VERIFIED — specific to this (model, GPU) pair**

1. **Nobody has run Qwen3.8-27B on an HGX B300.** vLLM lists `b300` in three variants'
   `supported_hardware` and ships `hw/b300.json`, but B300 is absent from
   `meta.hardware`'s verified set; SGLang's cookbook has no B300 cell at all. GB300 —
   the same `sm_103` die, 288 GB and 11 % higher clocks — is verified on both.
   *Settle it:* `sglang.bench_serving --dataset-name random --random-input-len 8192
   --random-output-len 1024` on a p6-b300, then GSM8K.
2. **All of §3 and §4 are `est.`** No tok/s, TPOT or TTFT figure for this model exists on
   any datacentre GPU. The §3.3 proxy is a different model (397 B MoE) on the right GPU.
3. **FA4 vs FP8 KV on vLLM is an unresolved either/or.** vLLM's FA4 hd256 2-CTA kernel
   excludes quantized KV; every published recipe pins FP8 KV; the silent fallback is FA2
   ([flash-attention.md §16.3](../../cross-cutting/flash-attention.md)). Nobody has measured
   either kernel on `sm_103`. *Settle it:* read the selected backend from the server log,
   then A/B `--kv-cache-dtype auto` vs `fp8` on TTFT at 128 K.
4. **No attention-kernel TFLOPS figure exists for `sm_103`, for any kernel.** The planning
   band is 1.0×–1.4× the B200 numbers ([gpus/b300.md §5](../../gpus/b300.md)).
5. **NVIDIA's "2× attention performance" claim has no caveat footnote and no measurement.**
   Footnote 3 on the HGX table is the comparison baseline *"vs. NVIDIA Blackwell"*
   ([gpus/b300.md §3](../../gpus/b300.md)). Because 16 of 64 layers here do full attention
   at head_dim 256 and 48 do a softmax-free recurrence, even a real 2× SFU uplift would move
   less of this model than of a pure-attention one.
6. **B300 MBU/MFU is unpublished.** METHODOLOGY's 0.50–0.70 band is a guess; Blackwell
   software moves fast enough that any measured MFU has a short shelf life
   ([serving-optimizations.md §4.4](../../cross-cutting/serving-optimizations.md)).
7. **Which NVFP4 export to run on B300 is contradicted across sources.** vLLM's recipe
   restricts `nvidia/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4` to `rtx_pro_6000` / `dgx_spark_gb10`
   ([inference-engines.md §6.4](../../cross-cutting/inference-engines.md)), while
   [quantization-formats.md §9.4](../../cross-cutting/quantization-formats.md) records that
   the same export was *"tested on **GB300** with vLLM"* and SGLang measured all five
   checkpoints on GB300 ([architecture.md §8.2](./architecture.md)). Both cited; the safe
   B300 choice is `Inferact/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4` (26.38 GB), at the cost of 4.46 GB.
8. **The `trtllm_mha` SM103 hang and its unavailable workaround.**
   [sglang#21904](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/issues/21904) (closed via #21906)
   hung *any* model at high concurrency on (G)B300 with the auto-selected `trtllm_mha`; its
   recommended fix — `--attention-backend flashinfer` — is **illegal for hybrid-GDN models on
   SM100/SM103**. Confirm your build routes to `triton` or `fa4` and re-test at
   concurrency ≥ 256.
9. **The `sm_103` FA4 CuTeDSL assertion.**
   [sglang#25564](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/issues/25564) — *"Only SM 10.x and
   11.x are supported"* on `lmsysorg/sglang:v0.5.12-cu130` with a `qwen3_5` model on B300,
   because `sm_110f` sorted below `sm_103` in the cutedsl enum. Fixed upstream
   ([flash-attention#2572](https://github.com/Dao-AILab/flash-attention/pull/2572)); verify
   your image carries the bump. More generally: `sm_100a`-only binaries **do not load on
   B300** — check with `cuobjdump --list-elf lib*.so | grep -c sm_103`
   ([gpus/b300.md §11.2](../../gpus/b300.md)).
10. **FlashInfer on `sm_103a` is *"architecture-gated but not yet signoff-qualified"*** — and
    it is the GDN prefill/decode path this model relies on
    ([gpus/b300.md §7](../../gpus/b300.md), [flash-attention.md §9.4](../../cross-cutting/flash-attention.md)).
11. **vLLM has no Blackwell CI at all** (L4 and H100 only); its own RFC states FA4 and full
    native NVFP4 paths are *"not yet qualified"* ([gpus/b300.md §7, §11.7](../../gpus/b300.md)).
    Pin versions; test your exact checkpoint on your exact node.
12. **vLLM's state-slot count `S` is unpublished.** Every fit cell in §1 and every
    feasibility verdict in §3–§4 is engine-specific until it is known; SGLang publishes
    5 / 4 / 3 / 1 by strategy, vLLM publishes nothing
    ([architecture.md §5.4](./architecture.md)). *Settle it:* boot at a fixed
    `--max-num-seqs` and divide the reported mamba-cache allocation by 78.45 MB.
13. **ReplaySSM's gain on this model is unmeasured** — and §3.2 shows it is aimed at 41 % of
    the decode step at the S1 operating point.
14. **No prefix-hit-rate → TTFT curve exists for any hybrid-GDN model**, where a hit must
    also restore a recurrent-state checkpoint ([architecture.md §5.6](./architecture.md)).
    §4.2's numbers are `est.` from FLOP scaling.
15. **4-bit KV on this model is untried.** FlashInfer's NVFP4 paged-KV decode/prefill merged
    for SM100/SM103 on 2026-09-18 — one day before this research date — so no one has
    exercised it here ([flash-attention.md §9.4](../../cross-cutting/flash-attention.md)).
16. **Cold-start time on a B300 host is unmeasured** (§5).
17. **Tool-call parser disagreement carries over**: vLLM pins `qwen3_xml`, SGLang pins
    `qwen3_coder`, for the same chat template ([architecture.md §1.4](./architecture.md)).

**Accuracy caveats of the executed precision**

- **No NVFP4 or FP8 accuracy table is published on `nvidia/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4`** — the card
  lists benchmark *names* (GPQA Diamond, Terminal-Bench, AA-LCR, MMMU-Pro, SciCode, IFBench)
  **without scores** ([quantization-formats.md §9.4](../../cross-cutting/quantization-formats.md)).
  That is the single biggest accuracy gap for this deployment.
- What *is* published for nearby builds: **RedHatAI INT4** recovers 98.5–101.1 % of BF16
  across IFEval, MMLU-Pro, GSM8K-Platinum, MATH-500, GPQA-Diamond and AIME 2025; **AMD Quark
  AWQ INT4** recovers 97.7 % on GSM8K and 95.6 % on Wikitext PPL; **SGLang's 202-cell
  v0.5.19 GSM8K sweep across every BF16/FP8/NVFP4 cell lands in 93.18–95.15 %** — but that
  sweep covers RTX 5090 / RTX PRO 6000 / DGX Spark only, **not GB300 and not B300**
  ([architecture.md §9.3, §10.1](./architecture.md)).
- **FP8 KV is an accuracy choice, not just a memory one.** GQA with only 4 KV heads has
  little redundancy; every recipe pins it and nobody has published a KV-dtype ablation for
  this model ([architecture.md §5.5](./architecture.md)).
- **bf16 GDN state is *"an accuracy gate"*** in SGLang's own words — validate it for your
  workload, and note SGLang's warning that with speculation fp32 sometimes wins and
  sometimes loses (NVFP4 + EAGLE: 152.9 vs 144.5 tok/s/user; FP8 + EAGLE: 106.3 vs 116.1),
  *"measure both for your quantization"* ([architecture.md §5.4](./architecture.md)). On
  B300 the bf16 choice is additionally load-bearing for the GDN prefill kernel (§1.1), so
  an accuracy failure here has a throughput cost too.
- **`--enable-linear-replayssm-spec` with an explicit non-fp32 `--mamba-ssm-dtype` logs a
  state-drift warning at boot** ([architecture.md §8.6](./architecture.md)). SGLang's
  bf16 + EAGLE cells run with that warning accounted for; yours should be too.

**Non-technical risk**

- **Price, not silicon, decides the B300 verdict.** At `low` $7.40 (Hyperstack) B300 is
  34.1 GB of HBM per $/hr — 1.7× H100's ([gpus/b300.md §12(c)](../../gpus/b300.md)). At
  OCI's `high` $15.00 it is 17.9 GB per $/hr, *below* H100. The 2.03× spread between the
  two published rows moves every cost conclusion in §4.
- **B200 may be the better buy for this model.** Bandwidth is identical (8 TB/s), FP8 is
  identical, the FP4 gain measured on the same architecture family is 1.05–1.17× (§3.3), and
  this model needs 21.92 GB — so B300's +48.9 % capacity buys concurrency the model only
  partly converts into throughput (325 vs 205 at 8 K per GPU,
  [architecture.md §10.2](./architecture.md)). At `b200` `low` $6.00 vs `b300` `low` $7.40,
  [architecture.md §11.3](./architecture.md) already ranks B200 NVFP4 at $0.215/1M output
  ahead of B300's $0.219 at flat 8 K context. **Buy B300 for this model when you need the
  128 K–1 M concurrency or the 8-replica density; buy B200 when you need $/token at 8 K.**
