## Proposed rulings (unnumbered)

- D8 ledgers: a label event is one per (provider, key) for ever under the provider's own dataset; a synthetic annotation
  is never ground truth and a human one names its reviewer, at publication; an external run is created only from its own
  published record and moves by CAS along the frozen machine; each submit key holds PROVIDER_USD once on the payer's D6J
  budget and settles once (unknown cost = the whole hold), never both settled and released; a teacher run is consented
  per sample (external_judging AND training, snapshotted, re-checked unchanged before egress).
- Checkpoint events and decisions are keyed per provider; the first rejection reason and the first decision stand.
- A release proposal is filed at the fence the page showed, one pending per revision, and approved only through D9's CAS.
- Routing eligibility = a current provider_sharing grant to the policy's provider, organization not suspended; default deny;
  a serving ref's uuid is its serving version and its pin is `<alias>@<revision_label>`.
- The Lab control service logs in as `infrx_lab_control` (≤ 10 connections, Lab RPCs only).
