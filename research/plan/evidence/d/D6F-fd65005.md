## Design (FEEDBACK-ACK / DUR-RLS)
- One transaction per write: feedback row + `infrx.idempotency` row + one `feedback_projection` outbox event; the acknowledgment follows the commit, a crash after commit replays to the same row (no second row/event).
- Ownership: `infrx.jobs (request_id, org_id)` — no trace projection involved (none exists in the check world); another org's or an unknown request is `not_found` (write and read).
- Provenance: body limited to `{name, value, comment}` (any author/channel/marker/calibration key → `invalid_request` in SQL and by `FeedbackSubmission extra=forbid` in Python); `author_role` is always `customer` on accept (R31); `by_operator` and channel are the server's; labels only through `label_calibration` (operator; tenant = the job's, R26; audited, R34).
- Duplicates: per-scope advisory lock + existence check → a concurrent duplicate waits and replays the first row; a changed payload or a key the other operation made is `idempotency_conflict` (R54).
- Separate flag from judge (09 §D6F): `feedback`; missing/disabled → fail closed.
