## (2) WR-LAB-API-4 (optional): not done, and why

`PgServing` (lab_traces) reads `infrx.serving_versions` directly. It is not closed. 0007 grants `service_role` SELECT
+ INSERT on the whole registry, and A3's `PgRegistry`/`PgCatalogDirectory`, admission's pin resolution and T2I's

this select (0043), and the login check above runs `PgServing.SQL` on it. An RPC would add a second read path with no
consumer. It is due in the same change that closes 0007's registry read to `service_role`.

## (3) Minors

- **`c3l_double_click_twice` (d6j, survived once under key b1 under full-suite load, LAB-SQL-INTEGRATION-a6b4bbb.md:63):
  made deterministic.** `check_a_concurrent_double_click_queues_one_run` used to depend on timing, so under load the
  second click could find the committed row by its own lookup. Now:
  - the first click inserts and holds its transaction open.
  - the second click starts, and the check waits until `pg_stat_activity` shows it WAITING on a lock (the unique key
    of the uncommitted row). Only then does the first click commit. The check asserts the wait happened.
  - Without `on conflict (run_id) do nothing`, the second click therefore always hits the unique key, and its answer differs from the first click's.
  - Killed 3/3 in separate processes, and within the full d6j list.
- **WR-LSQ3 leftovers (D8-1079c32.md "Open").**
  - lab_traces on the control login: traced. `_lab_traces` builds retention from ClickHouse + S3 only
    (`ClickHouseRetentionStore`, the projections, `S3ObjectStore`). Its one PG read is `PgServing.SQL` on
    `serving_versions`, which answers on the login (checked above). Closed.
  - `release_eligible`'s purpose, the judge door's `max = 5`, and `shadow_limit` without an RPC: these are product
    rulings or the owners' inputs, not SQL defects. Unchanged, still open for the coordinator.
- **SR-R1-1 against the 0043 login roles.** `check_the_router_functions_are_the_runtime_logins_alone` runs
  `PgRoutingReleases.active/eligible` on a `set session authorization infrx_runtime` session (the runtime's own role,
  no `set role`) and gets `(None, False)`. On `infrx_lab_control` it gets `InsufficientPrivilege`. The three functions
  are SECURITY DEFINER, so EXECUTE is all the runtime needs. The control login is a member of no role (0043
  re-revokes), so it cannot inherit them.

## Proposed rulings (unnumbered)

- The Lab control service's login reads the catalog listings: SELECT only, under its own RLS policy. The listings are
  written only by the publication and rollback CAS RPCs.
- L3's `ControlReads` are plain registry selects: a provider's own rows, oldest first (ties by id). An endpoint's alias
  is the newest listing that names a deployment on that endpoint.
- A concurrency check proves its race happened: the second party is observed waiting before the first commits.
