row. The first three are the primary classes; `DERIVED` and `CORRECTED` are

  which the row cites. It is never a fourth kind of fact: the underlying
  observation is in §Commands, the inference is the row's own.

  and that has since been re-observed. It carries the new OBSERVED time, says
  what the old claim was and why it was wrong, and the §Verification log records
  the correction. Nothing is silently overwritten.

A row whose class changed because a later pass observed what an earlier pass had
not says so inline ("OBSERVED … — was HISTORICAL CLAIM"); that is a class change,
