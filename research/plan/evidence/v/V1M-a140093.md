## Blockers

1. **LW2 gate not met: the `packages/shared` C0 QueryPort + trace-formatting copy (LW0 item 7) is not on the base.** `packages/shared` holds only `contracts/lab/`. Its exports and the Lab lockfile are the coordinator's (R154), and `@infrx/shared` is an injected `file:` copy in `apps/lab/node_modules/.pnpm`, so a new shared file also needs `pnpm install` under the coordinator's lockfile.
2. **No read path from the Lab to trace rows under L2 grants.** The Lab server holds only the anon key (R156/0030) and has no PostgreSQL or ClickHouse client in its dependencies. The explorer reads `ConsoleServices.traces(session, query)`, which is consumer-org tenancy (the App's C1 QueryPort over ClickHouse). V1M must read as a *provider*: the provider's own deployments' requests, only where the grantor org's current grant allows the category for `provider_sharing` (`contracts/v2 authorize_content_read`), with T3's deletion tombstones and expiry applied. No such port exists on any branch (C2/C2-RPC are LW3).
