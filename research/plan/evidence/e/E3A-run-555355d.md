## 2. `operator-controls` (was NOT RUN[operator-action])

- **Lanes:** it needs `U3` and `D10` (0025), both `implemented`.
- **Cell:** it sits under CONSOLE-FLOWS, and so under APP-JOURNEY.

The journey, in order:

1. **An operator.** A fresh individual signs up and verifies through the edge. The control API sets `profiles.is_operator = true` on the clone. That is the flag 0001's `is_operator()` and the App's session read; the App cannot set it.
2. **One U3 action with a reason, in the browser.** On `/admin`, "Suspend or restore" is filled with user A's organization id, Suspend, and a reason. The reason deliberately names another principal: `… on behalf of operator:00000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000000`. Then Apply is clicked.
   - The App's server action calls `public.operator_set_suspension` through the edge and PostgREST with the operator's JWT (R143).
   - The form reports "Committed. The change is recorded once in the audit trail."
3. **Audit.** Exactly one new `infrx.audit_entries` row for that organization: `admin_set_suspension`, `actor_principal = operator:<the signed-in operator's uid>`, the typed reason, and key `app-operator:suspension:<k>`. The actor is not the principal named in the reason.
4. **The individual's view.** In a separate browser context, user A signs in:
   - `/settings` shows "This account is suspended".
   - An external `POST /v1/chat/completions` with A's key gets **403 `org_suspended`**, and no job is admitted.
5. **Replay of the same key.** The same key `<k>` is sent again through the committed RPC under the operator's JWT, via the control API's `/operator-rpc` (a fresh edge session, then the journey PostgREST). The answer is `200 {"replayed": true, "suspended": true}`, and there is no new audit row.
6. **The same RPC under A's own JWT** is refused **403** by the database's `is_operator()`. Nothing moves, and the organization stays suspended.
7. **Restore.** The form is used again (Restore, with a reason). Exactly one more row appears, again with the operator's actor. The organization is unsuspended, and A's `/settings` no longer says suspended.
8. **The operator's `/admin` audit trail** shows the reason and `operator:<uid>`.

Why the replay goes through the control API: the form holds its key in state, never renders it, and rotates it after a commit (U3's `nextKey`). The browser therefore cannot resend a committed key. The key comes back from the audit row, and the RPC is the same one the form calls.

- **Fails-before (tree):** at the base the check skipped `NOT RUN[operator-action]` whatever the manifest said.
- **Fails-before (behaviour):** a mutant in the probe had `infrx.console_operator` take the actor from a principal named in `p_reason` (the D10 mutant `d10_operator_actor_from_an_argument` in spirit). The check went **FAIL**: "one audit row, the operator's", expected `operator:f559bbd9-…`, received `operator:00000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000000`.

## 3. `expired-display` (U4, R141; new)

- **Lanes:** it needs `U4`.
- **Cells:** DUR-OUTPUT and CONSOLE-FLOWS.
- **Placement:** it runs after `expired-result` and before `revoke-key`.

The check, in order:

1. User A opens `/usage/<async request>`. Before expiry, `main pre` shows exactly the async job's result text, the text the API returned in `async-poll`.
2. A soft navigation follows (the page's own `/usage` link, Next's client router).

4. **Back** (`page.goBack()`, Next's router-cache restore):

   - **no `main pre`** is present.
   - the result text appears nowhere in `main`.
5. **Reload:** the server's note "The result expired at …" (read from the store's `result_available`) is shown, with no `pre` and no content. The request id is still shown, because the metadata stays.
6. `GET /usage/<id>/result` with the page's cookies answers **410** with `Cache-Control` containing `no-store`.
7. The clock is reset in `finally`.
