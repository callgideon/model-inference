## Open issues

* **Span is a fragment, not a cut.** The gateway's `video_url` is exactly `{url}` (R58); N1
  stores the whole source object and the span. A fetcher drops `#t=`, so the engine gets the
  whole object: a source longer than `MAX_VIDEO_SECONDS` is refused by the gateway even when
  the span is within 82 s, and two spans of one clip are the same video to the model. Closing
  that needs either N1 storing the cut clip (decode, ffmpeg) or a gateway span field (frozen
  contract change). Fixture clips (synthetic bytes) are not decoded here.
* **Re-sign under one key.** A 402 give-back re-runs the same attempt key with a newly signed
  URL; the gateway digest includes the URL, so if a 402 ever recorded the key the replay would
  be `idempotency_conflict` (marked `ponytail:` in `_call`).
* `tests/i/lab_eval/test_drills_pg.py` (i5 key) not run: not this lane's key.
