## Observations

- **O1: why port 57080 collides (WR-E3L-2).** The fake vLLM (E3C's `world.composed`, not this
  lane's file) binds 57080, and `bind_retried` gives it 10 × 2 s = 20 s. A port watcher during
  mutant attempt 2 (`attempts/port-57080-watch.log`) saw a server-side `FIN-WAIT-2` socket on
  127.0.0.1:57080 last at least 24 s after a case ended. This host's `tcp_fin_timeout` is 60 s,
  and on Linux `SO_REUSEADDR` does not cover FIN-WAIT-2. So a case that starts within about
  60 s of a half-closed engine connection can exhaust its 20 s of retries. That matches the 8
  consecutive failures in the reproduction.
  - Suggested to E3C's owner: a `bind_retried` budget of at least `tcp_fin_timeout`, or have
    the fake vLLM close its keep-alive connections on stop.
  - This lane did not change it (not owned). The reservation half of WR-E3L-2 stays
    user-held.
- **O2.** `scenarios_publish.py` has a pre-existing unused `errors` import in l03a. Ruff
  flags it (F401), and it was already present at the base. It was not changed, to keep the
  diff to its purpose.
