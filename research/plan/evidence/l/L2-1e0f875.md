### Per finding
- **0-F1, store clock.** `AccessStore.db_now()` (`select infrx.now()`). `LabAccess` has no clock
  parameter and no `_utcnow`. Each decision reads the rows first and then `db_now()`, so a
  revocation or grant committed before the read is never in that clock's future (R7/R79).
  `FakeAccessStore(now=...)` is the injected clock that stands for the database's. The new case
  `..._revocation_and_grants_are_judged_on_the_store_clock` freezes the store clock at 2099 while
  the process runs in 2026. A grant and a membership revoked at store time T are refused at T,
  and a grant written at T authorizes at T. The case is killed by `clock_is_the_process_clock`
  (the handback's behaviour: `datetime.now()` at the three reads) on the fake, and by
  `pg_clock_is_the_process_clock` on PostgreSQL.
- **0-F2, viewer and grant history.** Chosen policy: `grant_history` needs developer+
  (`manage_dev_deployment`). A current viewer gets `Forbidden` whatever grantor is named, so the
  refusal is no enumeration oracle; a non-member still gets `NotFound`. The case
  `..._a_viewer_reads_aggregates_and_never_content_or_grants` covers it: the viewer reads
  aggregates, and gets 403 on content and on the history of C1 and of C2. Both choices are
  killed: `history_open_to_viewer` (the old behaviour) and `aggregates_need_developer`. The RLS
  line in WR-L2-1 is withdrawn (below).
