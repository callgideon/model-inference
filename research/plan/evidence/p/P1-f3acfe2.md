## Design (P1.a/b/c)
- **P1.a import.** Each pipeline row `{sample_id, method: human|model, method_version, label, annotator?, model?, prompt?, confidence 0..1?, spans [[start_ms,end_ms]]?}` becomes one D7 `lab.annotation.1`: method `imported` (a pipeline's human label) or `synthetic` (a model's), `ground_truth` false, state `submitted`, `evidence_ref` = the sample's own source ref (the original evidence is linked, the N1 content object is never touched), `rubric_ref` pinned; the provenance and the pipeline's own method word ride inside the opaque `label` = `{value, declared_method, provenance}` under the record digest. The annotation id is derived from (dataset, row), so a re-import is D7's replay (same refs, no new events). Rejections are reported by row number: `missing_evidence` (no such sample), `grant_not_current` (the access gate now), `forged_ground_truth` (any ground-truth claim), `bad_mapping` (unknown method/key, no version/label, bad confidence or span, a number the two halves read differently).
- **P1.b review.** D8's append-only log holds one event per idempotency key for ever: `label:<ref>`, `assign:<dataset>:<sample>:<reviewer>`, `review:<ref>` (accepted/rejected), `supersede:<ref>`, `adjudicate:<dataset>:<sample>`. Roles are read at each call on the L2 store's clock: an administrator (`manage_members`) assigns a current developer-or-above (`run_evaluation`) under one rubric version; a review needs the reviewer's own assignment under the rubric it cites and a still-current membership; moves follow F3's annotation machine once (a replay is the same event, another decision `IdempotencyConflict`, an undeclared move `StateConflict`). A **correction** rejects the pipeline label (which still resolves unchanged) and publishes the reviewer's `human` ground-truth label on the same evidence with `provenance.corrects`. A **disagreement** is ≥ 2 live (submitted/accepted) labels on a sample with different values; an **adjudication** is one per sample, by an assigned reviewer who wrote or reviewed none of the disputed labels: a `human` label with `provenance.adjudicates`, the disputed ones superseded (accepted) or rejected (submitted), all kept; a replay settles exactly what the first settled. `select` publishes a derived version of the chosen samples the access gate allows now, each in its original split, naming its parent.
- **P1.c export.** `sft.1` lines `{prompt, completion}` or `preference.1` lines `{prompt, chosen, rejected}` (a label that is not exactly chosen+rejected is `bad_mapping`). An example is a **train** sample (validation and holdout are omitted by split) whose accepted labels agree (else `disagreement`), allowed by the training gate now (`grant_not_current`), and not a holdout sample of this version or any ancestor by sample id, content digest or group key (`holdout_descendant`). Labels of ancestor versions carry to the same sample ids. Unlabelled samples are not examples. Bytes and record are write-once under `lab/<provider>/label-exports/<uuid>/`; the record carries per-example lineage (label refs, methods) and every omission with its reason.

## Oracles
- PIPELINE-LINEAGE: provenance/declared method/evidence kept on import; original record unchanged after correction and adjudication; synthetic never relabelled (accept keeps `synthetic`, no ground truth); forged reviewer (viewer, revoked, unassigned, wrong rubric version, developer assigning) denied with the log unchanged; a membership revoked after assignment cannot review; replayed correction/adjudication is the same label; export lineage names label refs and methods.
- DATA-SPLIT: export is train only; holdout descendants (moved id, copied content, same group) omitted; select keeps splits.
- DATA-RIGHTS: import/select through the access gate, export through the training gate, both read now (sharing-only grant imports but exports nothing; revoked grant rejects labels).

## Open issues
- The F3 annotation record has no provenance fields; P1 packs them in the opaque `label` (`{value, declared_method, provenance}`). A contract revision (R163) could promote them; proposed as a ruling instead.
- A reviewer's human label uses `method_version: "review"`; the rubric version is its `rubric_ref`.
- Exports are one object per export (`ponytail:` note in code); N2-style parts when label exports outgrow a request.

## Proposed ruling (next free R173+; propose, never number)
- P1 labels: a pipeline row is published `imported` (human) or `synthetic` (model), never `human` or ground truth; its provenance rides in the opaque label `{value, declared_method, provenance}`; only a review by a current developer-or-above, assigned by an administrator under the rubric version it cites, writes a `human` ground-truth label (a correction or a one-per-sample adjudication by a reviewer independent of the disputed labels), and every superseded or rejected label is kept; review state is D8's append-only log, one event per key; a training export is train-only, accepted and agreeing labels, the training gate now, and excludes any holdout sample of the version or its ancestors by id, content digest or group key.
