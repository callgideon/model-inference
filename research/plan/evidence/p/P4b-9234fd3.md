## Behaviour (each decision has a named mutant)

- **Dry run** (`POST teacher-batches`, `run_evaluation`): the body is `{batch_id, dataset_ref, rubric_ref, teacher_model, prompt_version, payer_ref, budget_usd (R159 USD), chunk_size 1..200}`. The provider's own payer and a dataset it holds are required; anything else is a 422 (R183). The batch is stored write-once under `lab/<p>/teacher-batches/<batch_id>/batch.json`: the same body again returns the stored batch, and another body is a 409. The answer is P2's `plan` as of now. It shows each chunk's samples and its reservation at the rate in force, the cost ceiling against the budget (`within_budget`), the price version, the holdout left out and the samples not permitted to leave. Nothing is reserved or sent.
- **Approve** (`POST teacher-batches/{id}/approve`). The actor needs membership only. The batch is looked up first (404 whatever the role), and only then is `manage_members` checked (403, administrator only). An unpriced ceiling, or one over the budget, is a 409. Live mode off (`judge_mode != live`) is a 503. Then the approval `{approved_by, approved_at}` (database clock, first wins) is recorded, and P2's `run_batch` runs: one D6J/D8 reservation per chunk against the payer, then J2's one egress path. A second approval (a double click) resumes and sends nothing twice.
- **Read-back**: each chunk shows its ledger state (`unreserved` = never reserved; `ambiguous` included), its hold, the cost as reported (null = unknown), the sent count and the per-item failures from `PgTeacherLedger.failures`.
- **Lab**:
  - The training page gains a "Teacher labelling" section. It shows a table of batches: teacher, the USD budget and payer, the ceiling, and the plan with a note that the returned labels are "imported as synthetic (model-generated), never ground truth".
  - Each chunk's copy says what its records say. An ambiguous chunk is "held; never resent, only looked up". A stopped chunk is "not reserved: the batch stopped…". A cost is as reported or "unknown… never estimated". Failures are counted by reason, and an unknown reason is shown as "not recognised".
  - The approve form (hidden `batchId` = the record's) appears only for an administrator, on a dry run within its budget.
  - The dry-run form carries `batchId = randomUUID()` minted at render.
  - If the teacher port is unavailable, the section says "not enabled" rather than blanking the page.
  - The actions refuse a missing role or a malformed shape before the service is asked. The payer and dataset must be this provider's, and the batch id is never minted by the action.

## Ruling proposal (unnumbered)

LAB-TEACHER-APPROVAL:
- A teacher batch reaches the Lab only through `/lab/v1/pipelines/teacher-batches` as the signed-in user.
- Its `batch_id` is minted when the form renders and is write-once: the same body returns the stored batch, another body is a 409.
- A batch is a dry run until approved: the plan, each chunk's reservation and the cost ceiling are shown against the batch's PROVIDER_USD budget and its named payer, and nothing is reserved or sent.
- Approval is addressed to the batch (R183: 404, then the role). Only an administrator (`manage_members`, until a spend capability exists) approves. An unpriced ceiling, or one over the budget, is refused (409). Live teacher submission off is a 503.
- The approval is recorded before any egress. A repeat approval resumes and never resends.
- An ambiguous chunk stays held and is only looked up.
- A cost is as reported or unknown, never estimated.
- Returned labels are synthetic, never ground truth.

## Open issues

- The approve capability is `manage_members` (the only administrator-only capability the Lab vocabulary has besides `propose_publication`); a dedicated spend capability would be a contracts change.
- The ceiling is re-priced on every read and at approval (the rate in force). A rate change between the dry run and approval is caught by the approval's budget check; the dry-run record itself stores no price.
- The route does not re-check that a chunk's ledger row belongs to the provider (the run ids are uuid5 of a provider-scoped batch id and that provider's sample ids).
