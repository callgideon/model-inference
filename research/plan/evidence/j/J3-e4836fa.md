## Decisions (each killed by a named mutant)

- Truth = `calibration_label` rows only (R43: operator-authored, `by_operator`; C3F/R31 is the only writer); customer thumbs/ratings/corrections, judge rows and operator ordinary comments are counted under `not_a_calibration_label`, never truth. Another org's or rubric version's labels are excluded and never exemplars; `unusable` is not a verdict; two verdicts for one sample are `conflicting_labels` (both dropped).
- Results: rejected, other-version, limited (no-media, R56) and duplicate results are counted, never paired; the first result per sample wins.
- Kappa on `overall_pass` vs correct/incorrect (`partially_correct` is ordinal-only, counted `partial_not_binary`); Spearman rho per trusted criterion (`06` §3.9: groundedness, relevance) vs incorrect<partial<correct, average ranks for ties.
- Uncertainty: kappa large-sample SE, 95% interval clamped to [-1,1]; rho Fisher z with the 1.06 factor (n>3). `met` only when the lower bound >= 0.6, `not_met` when the upper bound < 0.6, `inconclusive` otherwise; `insufficient` under 30 pairs; `not_computed` for a constant side. `calibrated` only when kappa AND every trusted rho are `met` (kappa alone never calibrates).
- Exemplars: sample ids (no content) of this org's kappa disagreements.
- WR-V3-1 adapter: provider from the session actor only; viewer = `denied` with no call; malformed id = `unavailable` with no call; 42501/P0002 = `denied`, anything else = `unavailable`; one unreadable row fails the whole read; a no-media pass on a media rubric or a `calibrated` claim with labels<required / no agreement / no interval is refused (fail closed).
