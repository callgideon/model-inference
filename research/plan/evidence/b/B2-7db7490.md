## What B2 does
- **B2.a pairing**: through H1's `compare` (UNIVERSE provider/dataset/evaluator/environment/max_cases must match, else refused; FACTORS serving/harness/seed → `rerun` / `single_factor` / `multifactor` only with the protocol's `multifactor_tag`). Exact case ids; a duplicate case or one outside the frozen universe is refused.
- **B2.b scoring**: observed per run over the whole universe — mean with errors = 0 and missing / not comparable = 0 (dropping failures never helps), counts of missing, errors, not comparable; paired estimate over cases scored in both runs: mean diff, cluster-robust SE (`g/(g-1) Σ_g(Σ(d−d̄))² / n²`, cluster = the case's source, default the case) and Student t on g−1 df (Cornish-Fisher expansion, `t_quantile`); per-unit cost totals (CREDIT, PROVIDER_USD; typed `Credit`/`ProviderUsd`, never summed across; legacy USD or unitless refused) and per-unit deltas only where both runs paid in that unit; nearest-rank latency p50/p90/p99/max.
- **B2.c decision**: predeclared protocol, every threshold required (confidence, margin, min_cases, metric_source, required_slices with their own margin/min_cases; multifactor tag optional). Verdict `non_inferior` (low ≥ −margin), `inferior` (high < −margin), `uncertain`, `insufficient` (< min cases or < 2 clusters). Any inferior overall/required slice → `reject`; incomplete coverage or any uncertain/insufficient → `inconclusive`; else `accept`; `improved` only when the overall lower bound > 0. Report: observed facts apart from estimates (with `basis` = deterministic metric or teacher judgment), run refs, universe/protocol digests, own `report_digest`.

## Failure injections (EVAL-COMPARE)
- Hidden slice regression: 30 non-safety fixes and 4/5 safety breaks → overall improved, safety inferior, `reject`; a 0.01 safety loss inside the overall margin is still `inconclusive` under the slice's zero margin.
- Missing failures: a candidate returning 36/40 cases → mean 36/40, missing 4, `inconclusive` ("coverage 36/40"); an error scores 0 and pairs; a blocked baseline case is unpaired.
- Duplicate cases / unequal case sets: refused (duplicate, outside the universe); another dataset refused; untagged multifactor refused.
- Skewed source clusters: the same 12 gains are a confident win as 40 sources and not as 4 correlated ones (interval > 2× wider, exact to t(3)); one cluster is insufficient.
- Small samples: 8 < 10 → insufficient; a wide interval crossing the margin → uncertain; neither claims improvement.
- Units: CREDIT 80 and PROVIDER_USD 0.4 totalled apart, delta only in CREDIT; USD / unitless refused.
