## What B1 does
- **B1.a `freeze`**: parses the `lab.eval_run.1` record, refuses an evaluator spec whose canonical sha256 is not the run's `evaluator_ref` digest (R167: no evaluator table yet), validates the spec (metric `exact_match`, reference path, H1 per-case replay bounds), requires a CREDIT budget (no unbounded provider_dev spend), publishes and creates the run in D7 (scheduling gate = D7's provider_sharing check), resolves manifest and harness. `Frozen` carries the run ref, harness bytes, evaluator, `split_digest` (N2's function over the manifest splits, WR-N-4), the deterministic cases (first `max_cases` sample ids, R168) and the holdout cases. Re-freezing the same record replays (same ref, same cases).
- **B1.b `Runner.run`**: `concurrency` workers (`<worker>/<i>`) loop over D7 leases. Before each lease the run's recorded CREDIT is compared to the budget limit (stop `budget_exhausted`). Per attempt: attempt > `max_attempts` → failed `attempts_exhausted` without a call; access gate re-read (`accessible_samples`, provider_sharing) → failed `revoked` without a call; H1 `Replayer` runs the harness against the dev deployment; every model call is preceded by `heartbeat` (the fence: a cancel / lost lease stops spending) and keyed `attempt:<run>:<case>:<n>:<call>`; 429/5xx/transport are retried under the same key up to `dispatch_retries` (the endpoint replays, no second debit); a 402 stops the run for good with cases left pending/leased (unresolved, never dropped); a kill leaves the lease to expire, `lab_recover` returns the case, the next attempt is a new bounded paid attempt.
- **B1.c results**: one result per case under the run's evaluator (D7 enforces run × case × evaluator): status/comparable/reasons from H1, output, exact-match score (none for blocked/unsupported or a missing reference, with `missing_reference`), split, latency_ms, usage (calls, tokens); cost per attempt = sum of the dev rate card's debits (CREDIT). Infra failures (retries spent, final refusal, replay bound `bound:<b>`, revoked, attempts spent) fail the attempt with its cost. A finish refused by the fence is abandoned and its charge reported as `unrecorded` (no stale overwrite). Outputs over D7's 64 KiB body bound are scored then dropped with `output_too_large`.
- **`HttpDevEndpoint`** (L3 stand-in): OpenAI-compatible `/v1/chat/completions` with the endpoint-scoped provider_dev bearer, `Idempotency-Key` and `seed`; cost = frozen `RateCardSnapshot.debit(usage)` (the D5 settlement formula, records.py:607); 402 → `InsufficientCredit`, 429/5xx/transport → `DependencyUnavailable`, other → `InvalidRequest`; finite video refused until L3's media path.

## Drills (EVAL-DURABLE), fake world and real D7
- Worker killed after the charge, before the finish: attempt 1 `expired` with no recorded cost, attempt 2 finishes the case, one result; wallet shows both debits (gap visible, bounded by `max_attempts`); past `max_attempts` the case fails with no call.
- Lost answer (connection closed after the charge): resent under the same key, replayed, one debit (PG/HTTP).
- Durable cancel mid-batch (another connection's `lab_cancel_run`): the in-flight finish is refused by the fence and dropped; the fence before the next paid call stops a tool loop; no further calls; report `stopped: cancelled`, `abandoned`, `unrecorded`.
- Duplicate delivery: two concurrent runners + a third after completion → one result and one paid call per case; D7 CREDIT total == wallet debit.
- Exhausted dev wallet (starts at 0): stop after one 402, nothing done, wallet never negative; funded (operator allocation), lease expired, recovered → completes.
- Revoked data mid-run: every case `revoked`, zero calls; a grant version dropping provider_sharing also fails the case.
