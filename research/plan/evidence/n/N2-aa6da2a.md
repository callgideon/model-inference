## Design (N2.a/b/c)
- **Content-addressed versions (N2.a)**: a version is a `lab.dataset_manifest.1` published through D7 (ref = sha256 of the RFC 8785 bytes, one set of bytes per dataset/version); `derive(base?, add[])` publishes a NEW version naming all parents; nothing is edited.
- **Families and splits**: samples sharing a group key, a clip (`media_digest`, whatever the span/session) or a text equal up to case and whitespace are one family (union-find with path halving); a family is placed whole. Without a base: `sha256(seed "\n" least group key) mod 10000` over the policy's train/validation/holdout basis points (validated 0..10000). A family spanning several group keys is returned in `review` (near-duplicate review flags). `split_digest` = sha256 of the canonical splits.
- **Frozen holdout (N2.b)**: base samples keep their split; a new relative of a base family joins it, except the holdout — a new relative of a holdout sample is omitted (`related_to_holdout`) and new unrelated families are placed over train/validation only (a policy with no train/validation over a base is refused). A base whose declared splits put one family in two splits is refused (`LeakRefused`, every leak with its samples and splits). A repeated content digest is omitted (`duplicate`), so a re-import never changes the holdout.
- **Rights at every read (R160)**: derivation reads each parent through `accessible_samples(purpose=provider_sharing)` now and omits the rest (`grant_not_current`); a manifest stays resolvable metadata.
- **Exports (N2.c)**: gate `training` read now; the holdout never leaves (`holdout`); write-once parts of `part_items` JSONL items `{sample_id, split, content_digest, source_ref, grant_ref, record}` (record = the N1 content object minus the redacted top-level keys of `original`/`content`), then `export.json`: format, export id, dataset ref, `manifest_sha256`, schema, purpose, redacted keys, parts with sha256 and counts, `content_sha256`, every omitted sample with its reason, per-source lineage `{grant_ref, license, use_restrictions, ownership}` (from the N1 spec), `created_at`, `expires_at` (TTL 1 s..7 days). Resume reuses finished parts and refuses (`Conflict`) a part its inputs no longer produce; a finished export replays (and is never another dataset's); `cancel` is permanent (`Gone`); `read_part` refuses expired/cancelled/unknown and re-reads the training gate, dropping items whose grant was revoked or lost training since.

- DATA-SPLIT: clips of one video across sessions and near-duplicate texts never cross splits over 12 seeds, each family flagged; a base with a leaked family is refused with the leak listed; v2 keeps v1's holdout exactly, puts no new sample in it, moves a train relative into train, omits a holdout relative; a duplicate import omits all 25 rows and leaves the split digest unchanged (fake and real PG).
- DATA-RIGHTS: another provider's dataset `NotFound`; derivation omits a revoked source (reason visible) while its manifest still resolves; export omits revoked sources and never ships the holdout; a grant without `training` exports nothing; a read of an older export drops revoked/untrained items (fake and real `lab_revoke_access_grant`); TTL, export id (no path), cancel, expiry (inclusive end), unknown part.

- **0-N-C3 (N2, fixed).** In `derive`, a base sample the access gate no longer returns is still omitted (`grant_not_current`) but becomes an *anchor*: its group and near keys join the union-find and its base split is carried in `placed`; anchors are never published. A new sample related to an unreadable holdout sample (by group or by text up to case/spacing) is omitted as `related_to_holdout`. Two consequences, both intended: an anchor's content object is read (only to compute its near key; never published or returned); a base whose *unreadable* samples leak across splits is now `LeakRefused` like any other leaked base. `scratchpad/rv_n_repro3.py` now ends in D7 refusing an empty manifest (every new row was a holdout relative) instead of publishing train rows.
