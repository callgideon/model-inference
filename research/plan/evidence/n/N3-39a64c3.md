## Design (N3.a/b/c)
- **Selection through L2, T3 and C2 only.** `acting_provider` (WR-N-2), then L2 `authorize_content` for request AND response content (provider_sharing, the grantor's current grant, developer+); feedback joins only when the grant also names the `feedback` category. Per request (sorted, at most `MAX_SELECT` = 500 distinct, else `InvalidRequest` before any port): T3 `find_traces` empty → `missing_projection` (C2 never asked: a missing projection cannot substitute for permission; it also covers another grantor's request, a T3 deletion and metadata expiry); T3 content bound passed → `content_expired` (C2 not asked); C2 `sign` (bound to recipient, request, grant id) + `read` refusing with NotFound/Forbidden/Gone → `content_denied`; any other C2 error propagates and nothing is published. Nothing permitted → `InvalidRequest`, no source registered.
- **Sample content object** (RFC 8785, at `lab/<provider>/samples/<hex>` as R169): `{modality: structured, content, original (both the C2 document, untouched), annotation {imported, infrx.trace_sample.1}, trace {grantor, request, started_at, completed_at}, corrections [D6F rows: feedback_id, name, value, comment, author_role, channel, created_at - no author identity]}`. group key = request id; every sample in train (`versions.derive` places them). Source = `register_source(source_id = selection id, digest = sha256 of the canonical sorted [request, content digest] pairs, grant ref of the current grant version)`; one D7 publish (`derivation: import`).
- **Lineage (N3.b).** D7 already holds source → dataset → parents (manifests; derived versions copy sample ids and refs). N3 adds, write-once: `lab/<p>/lineage/samples/<sample>.json` {sample, selection, grantor, request, grant id, model, digest, `content_until` = T3's content bound} and the marker `lab/<p>/lineage/traces/<grantor>/<request>/<sample>`.
- **Denial (N3.c).** `permitted(store, objects, ref, provider, purpose, now)` = D7's current-grant samples − tombstoned − trace samples past `content_until`: the one gate derive, export, part read, a queued job and an external submission re-check (expiry is denied the instant it passes, no job needed). Tombstones `lab/<p>/lineage/tombstones/<sample>.json` {sample, grantor, request, reason, at} - never content - are write-once and permanent (a re-grant does not resurrect). `tombstone` = the push (T3 deletion / L2 revocation hook: WR-N3-2), paged by `limit`; `reconcile` = the pull over one page after a cursor: grant not current on the store clock → `grant_not_current` (logical only), T3 no rows → `deleted`, content bound passed → `content_expired`; for deletion and expiry only, then the sample copy is deleted (physical after retention). `status` = provenance/restriction view (N4); `export_evidence` = an export's items denied now, `recalled: False` and the not-recalled / not-unlearned note.

## Wiring requests
- **WR-N3-1 (C2 port, content lane):** N3 calls `Content.sign(*, provider_org_id, grantor_org_id, request_id, grant_id) -> str` and `Content.read(ref, *, provider_org_id) -> dict` (the trace's `{request, output}` document), refusing with `NotFound` (forged/altered/foreign ref), `Gone` (expired ref or content T3 no longer serves) or `Forbidden` (grant not current, or another grant), any other error = outage. If C2's adapter lands with another shape, N3 needs a ~5-line adapter at the call site; its integration rerun = `tests/n/lineage/test_lineage_pg.py` with C2's real adapter in place of `FakeContent`.
- **WR-N3-2 (push hooks, T3/L2 composition):** after `Retention.delete(org, request, …)` call `lineage.tombstone(objects, provider_org_id=<each provider with a marker under lab/*/lineage/traces/<org>/<request>/>, grantor_org_id=org, request_id=request, reason="deleted", at=now)`; after `lab_revoke_access_grant` call it with `reason="grant_revoked"` for the grant's recipient; run `lineage.reconcile` per provider on a worker schedule (I5) as the backstop. Flag default OFF like the rest of the Lab.
- **WR-N3-3 (callers of the gate, B1/P2/P3):** a queued or running job re-checks `lineage.permitted(store, objects, dataset_ref, provider_org_id=…, purpose=…, now=…)` before each lease/submission (this is the "revoked during queue wait / before external submission" guarantee for those lanes).

## Proposed ruling (coordinator numbers it; next free R173)
- N3 trace datasets: selection goes through the L2 content authorization (request + response, provider_sharing; feedback only under a feedback-category grant), T3 projection/retention and C2 signed refs only - a missing projection is an omission, never permission; a trace sample keeps the original request/output and span with D6F feedback appended as separate corrections without author identity; lineage per sample is write-once under `lab/<provider>/lineage/`; every dataset read, derivation, export, part read, queued job and submission uses `lineage.permitted` (current grant − tombstones − content past its bound); tombstones are permanent and hold no content; revocation is logical, deletion and expiry purge the copies after the tombstone; nothing delivered is claimed recalled or unlearned.
