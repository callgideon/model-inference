## s08 — what is bounded, and how

`intake.bounded(awaitable, bound_s)` answers `DependencyUnavailable` (503, `Retry-After`) once
`bound_s` passes, cancelling the call without waiting for it (a stalled socket or a thread must
not hold the answer). Applied to:

- every store/object call through `relay._dependency` (acceptance: lookup, stage, admission,
  the CREDIT recheck, attach, the resume path's reads; the jobs routes' owned reads, `db_now`,
  result reads, the events pre-read, DELETE) and discovery's catalog reads;
- the catalog resolution of every request (`routes/catalog.resolve`);
- media preparation (`prepare_request`: fetch, probe, source write) at
  `media_fetch_timeout_s + probe_timeout_s + 10 s` (40 s by default);
- the result read of a sync answer;
- the sync wait's and the SSE relay's polls: a stalled poll is retried, the wait still ends at
  the job's own deadline;
- the durable cancel: shielded, bounded; unconfirmed, it claims no state (504 at a sync
  deadline, 503 on DELETE) and `Relay.drain` still waits for it at shutdown.

`DEPENDENCY_BOUND_S = 20` s, past the pool's connect (5 s) plus statement (15 s) timeouts, so
a slow statement still gets the server's own refusal first. Worst single stall on the async
path: 40 s (preparation) < 45 s. A stall after the admission committed follows money-B2: the
job is left, not cancelled, and the same-key retry after recovery completes the attach and
answers that one job; a request without an `Idempotency-Key` that gets this 503 cannot be
matched to its job (the contract's reason for keys) - a text job admitted but never attached is
RV-05's zero-media gap, closed by D10's `admit_ready`.

## Wiring requests (unchanged from G7-2bbfe0e.md, plus)

- **WR-8 (I8, adapter side):** the route bound answers the client; the stalled call is
  cancelled on the loop but a psycopg connection or a botocore thread may stay stuck until
  the server answers. Client-side timeouts that survive a stalled server in the adapters
  (psycopg `options='-c statement_timeout'` does not fire on a paused server: a
  `tcp_user_timeout`/keepalive on the pool's connections; botocore `read_timeout` and
  `retries` totals under 20 s) keep the pool from filling with stuck connections; I8's pool
  telemetry should report waits.
- **Proposed ruling text (E3C's, as implemented):** "A request whose PostgreSQL, object store
  or catalog stops answering is answered a retryable 503 `dependency_unavailable` with
  `Retry-After` within 45 s (each dependency call is bounded at 20 s, media preparation at
  its fetch + probe timeouts + 10 s); an admission that committed before the stall is left
  for the same-key retry, which answers that one job; a stalled poll never extends a sync or
  SSE wait past the job's deadline."
