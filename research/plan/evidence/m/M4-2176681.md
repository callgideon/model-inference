## Limits

All limits of `M4-8179144.md` stand. Added or changed by this round:

1. **The header probe runs on the loop, one bounded walk per look** (≤ 7 looks to the cap,
   ≤ `MAX_ELEMENTS` headers each, a copy of at most `HEADER_PROBE_MAX` = 8 MiB, and no look at
   all after the first complete `moov` passed). It runs only with the built-in probe; an
   injected probe (M2's timeout drills) is the only one that decides.
2. A header-first clip whose leading `moov` ends past 8 MiB is no longer refused early; it is
   refused by the whole-object probe after the full read (no real clip in either corpus has a
   `moov` near that size).
3. **H4 (timing-dependent cases, pre-existing, not M4's):** `test_fetch.py::test_a_body_that_stalls_for_ever_is_bounded_by_the_backstop`
   (the 1.05 s real-clock backstop must fire inside `wait_for(…, 2.0)`) and
   `test_probe.py` line 170 (a hostile-length walk bounded at < 1.0 s wall). Both passed here;
   if either flakes on a loaded host, widening the outer bound (e.g. 5 s) keeps the invariant
   ("it returned"). No M4 test depends on host timing.
