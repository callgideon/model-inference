## F-5: the decision is (a). The admit_ready path skips the post-admission recheck

**Defect.** `Relay._admitted` ran on every fresh admission. In CREDIT it rechecked the pinned card and `check_capability` against the catalog's current revision. On the `admit_ready` path the marker is already committed at that point, so the worker may already have claimed, run and settled the job. A refusal there cancels the job. For a job already finished, `cancel` answers the committed outcome, and the refusal was then raised to the client anyway. E3C measured this: `unsupported_media` was sent for request `f61b82d3`, which ended `succeeded` and was debited.

**Refusal-reason table.** Every refusal the recheck can raise, compared with the admission transaction. The authority is `infrx.admit_ready` in `0019_upload_readiness.sql` and the `ReadinessStore.admit_ready` contract in `contracts/v2/lifecycle.py`.

| Recheck refusal (`relay.py` `_admitted`) | In the `admit_ready` transaction |
|---|---|
| `pins.rate_card_version != active_rate_card_version` → `invalid_request` | `expectation_mismatch` when `j.rate_card_version` is not the expectation's card. `Relay.__post_init__` refuses to construct unless `expectation.rate_card_version == active_rate_card_version` (CREDIT) and the regime matches. |
| pinned serving revision missing → `not_found` | `check_pinned_capability`: `v_cap is null` → `not_found` "the pinned serving revision is not in the catalog". This runs for CREDIT always, and for legacy when a catalog row exists. |
| `check_capability`: an input modality (text, video) outside `input_modalities` → `unsupported_media` | `check_pinned_capability`: every message part type (`string`→text, `video_url`→video), and media present ⇒ `video`, against the PINNED revision's `input_modalities` → `unsupported_media` (`param=messages`). |
| `check_capability`: stream mode without `stream_output` → `unsupported_parameter` | `check_pinned_capability`: `execution_mode = 'stream'` and `stream_output` not true → `unsupported_parameter` (`param=stream`). |
| `catalog.serving_revision` unavailable → 503 `DependencyUnavailable` (job left) | Not a refusal. On the `admit_ready` path it was only a 503 sent for a ready job. It is now gone with the recheck. |

The transaction covers every refusal the recheck can raise, checked against the pin at the moment of admission. A later change to the catalog is not a reason to refuse a job the transaction already accepted, and after the marker nothing can safely refuse it.

- **Fix** (`relay.py` `_admitted`): `if self.regime == CREDIT and self.readiness is None:`.
  - The recheck now runs only for the pre-D10 door (`jobs.admit`/`admit_credit`, no marker), where nothing is claimable until the refs are bound. That keeps G1R Limit 2 there.
  - The pre-admission ingress capability check is unchanged.
- **Ruling reference.** The brief cites R110, but R110 in `08-contracts-v1-encoding.md` §10 is E1C benchmark validity. The binding text used here is the `ReadinessStore.admit_ready` contract plus 0019 `check_pinned_capability`.
- **Proposed ruling** (coordinator numbers it): "A job the admission transaction made execution-ready is answered only its committed outcome (or its handle). Every refusal of the pinned card or capability happens inside that transaction; no later step of the acceptance may answer a refusal for it."

## F-6: the reconciliation reader on D10's monitor login; unset or refused means off, said once

**Fix.**
- `config.DeploymentSettings.monitor_database_url`: env `MONITOR_DATABASE_URL`, the name I8 planned (`durable.py`, `72-observe-install.sh`, D10 handoff §6).

  - Pinned in `DEPLOYMENT_EXPECTED`.
- `worker/__main__.py` `reconciliation_reader(deployment)`:
  - Unset: returns None and logs `reconciliation gauges disabled: no monitor login` once, at compose time.
  - Set: `PgReconciliation(connector(dsn, set_role=not pilot.dedicated_login(dsn)))`. A dedicated login gets no `SET ROLE`; its own `statement_timeout` and read-only defaults apply.
  - No grant was added to `infrx_runtime`.
- `worker/service.py` `_reconciled` handles SQLSTATE `42501` by setting the reader to None and logging one line: `reconciliation gauges disabled: the login may not read the reconciliation views (InsufficientPrivilege)`. This keeps a monitor login that lacks WR-W5F5-1 from producing an error on every tick. Any other failure keeps the S3 F4 rule: counted, logged and retried each tick.
