## CM-1: `credit-reads.ts` `rpcPage` on C0's rule (R146 App clause)

`rpcPage(page, call, parse)` now owns the call for both reads:

- a limit outside 1..100 (`MAX_PAGE_LIMIT` from `lib/contracts/types.ts`) is refused `invalid_request` before the call;
- `ask = Math.min(limit + 1, 100)`;
- `more = rows > limit || (ask === limit && rows === limit)`, so a full page at the cap carries a cursor.

`ledger` and `jobs` pass only their RPC call (`(ask) => client.rpc(…, { p_after, p_limit: ask, … })`).

## CM-2: the usage form judged by rendered markup

U1R-U06 no longer greps `usage-controls.tsx`. It renders `UsageControls` with `renderToStaticMarkup`. Filters are `{range: 7d, key, model: m, cursor: CURSOR-9, trail: [TRAIL-8]}`. It asserts:

- exactly one `<form>` with `action="/usage"`, not POST;
- the named fields, in order, are `range`, `key`, `model`;
- their values are `7d`, the key id and `m`;

`node --test` cannot load `.tsx` or `@/`. The test file therefore registers `module.registerHooks`: TypeScript's own `transpileModule` (already a devDependency) for `.tsx`, and resolution of `@/` and extensionless relative imports outside `node_modules`.
