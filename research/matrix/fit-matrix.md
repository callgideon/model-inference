[METHODOLOGY §2](../METHODOLOGY.md): linear attention / DeltaNet / KDA / Mamba cost **0 bytes per token** and instead a fixed `n_heads × d_k × d_v × B_state` (+ conv state) **per sequence slot**, multiplied by `S`, the number of slots the engine allocates per request.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Qwen3.8-27B](../models/qwen3827b/gb300.md) | **48 of 64** are GDN | **78,446,592 B bf16** (the [METHODOLOGY §8](../METHODOLOGY.md) pin); 153,944,064 B fp32 is the alternate | 5 (SGLang default) | **392.2 MB** (770 MB if the state is fp32) | At 8K the state term *dominates*: on GB300 TP1 the same GPU holds **340** sequences at `S=5` and **649** at `S=1` — a 1.9× swing from a scheduler constant, not from hardware |
| [Kimi-K3](../models/kimik3/gb300.md) | KDA | **428.6 MiB** per slot (fp32) → 221.6 MiB at `--mamba-ssm-dtype bfloat16` | 5 (SGLang) | **2.25 GB** | `--mamba-ssm-dtype bfloat16` is worth **+127 % concurrency at 8K** on an 8-GPU B300/GB300 replica. On B300 the levers move 8K seats 101 → 250 (`S=4`, bf16 state) or down to 68–70 under DSpark (`S=8`) |
| [Marlin-2B](../models/marlin2b/h100.md) | **18 of 24** GDN | **18.63 MiB** per sequence | 1 | 18.63 MiB | Small, but it is 100 % of the cost at short context; at 8K the KV term (12 KiB/token × 8,192 = 96 MiB) finally exceeds it |
| [DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash](../models/deepseek41f/b300.md) | *(not linear attention — sparse DSA)* | sliding-window ring **2,906,112 B = 2.77 MiB** FP8 (43 rings, MTP on; 2,703,360 B = 2.58 MiB with MTP off) / 5.38 MiB BF16 ⚠️ | 1 | 2.77 MiB | The BF16-container reading reduces every 4K column by ≈25 % ⚠️ |

**The practical consequence:** for the two hybrid models, *the dtype of a recurrent state and the value of `S` change capacity more than the KV-cache dtype does.* Both are engine flags, not hardware properties, and both are marked ⚠️ engine-dependent in the architecture docs.

## 6. Open questions

These are places where two documents in this tree disagree, or where `pairs.json` and the pair document it summarises disagree. Nothing here is resolved silently; both sides are cited.

### 6.1 The KV reading is not uniform across pair documents — ⚠️ TO BE VERIFIED

Every DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash pair has `num_key_value_heads = 1`, so under plain TP the latent KV is **replicated per rank**; under DP-attention / DCP it is **aggregated**. Different pair docs pick different readings for their headline number, and `pairs.json` inherits whichever they picked:

| Pair | `pairs.json` @8K / @128K | Which reading | The other reading, from the same doc |
|---|---|---|---|
| [deepseek41f/b300](../models/deepseek41f/b300.md) | 11,184 / 953 | replicated per rank — *"the binding reading"* | sharded: *"TP4 at 128K becomes 3,812 rather than 953 … **do not plan on it**"* |
| [deepseek41fnvfp4/b300](../models/deepseek41fnvfp4/b300.md) | **40,801 / 3,479** | **optimistic, KV sharded** | conservative/replicated TP4 = **10,199 / 869** |
| [deepseek41f/h200](../models/deepseek41f/h200.md) | **28,457 / 2,132** | **(A) node aggregate**, valid only with EP8 + DP-attention | **(R) 3,557 / 266** — and the doc's own conclusion is *"Use **(R)** for a vLLM TP8 deployment"*, backed by a measured `kv_cache_pool_tokens = 31,006,342` that reproduces (R)'s 266 almost exactly |
| [deepseek41f/a100](../models/deepseek41f/a100.md) | 15,473 / 1,159 | node aggregate — *legitimate here*, the recommended shape is TP4×DP2 with attention-DP | per-GPU 8-GPU Engram-resident row: 3,127 / 234 |

**Two sibling documents describing the same architecture on the same GPU disagree about the default.** `deepseek41f/b300` calls replication binding and prints 953 at 128K; `deepseek41fnvfp4/b300` leads with the sharded 3,479. Until someone prints `num_gpu_blocks` per rank on a plain-TP run, the replicated figures are the safe ones.

### 6.2 `pairs.json` records `deepseek41fnvfp4/b200` at the *minimum*, not the recommended, GPU count

`recommended_gpus` is **8**, but `max_concurrency_8k = 2,444` / `_128k = 208` are the **TP4** rows. At TP8 the same document gives **8,887 / 757** ([deepseek41fnvfp4/b200 §1.3](../models/deepseek41fnvfp4/b200.md)). Grid §2 above uses the TP8 figures in the "alt" line and flags the basis. This is the only pair of the 40 where the two disagree.

### 6.3 DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash's 890 B FP4 KV cache outside Blackwell — **RESOLVED 2026-09-19**

**The 890 B/token figure is architectural, not executed.** Both halves of the old dispute were right about different things, and separating them settles it:

* **The layout is real and byte-exact everywhere.** 720 B main (E2M1 + one E4M3 scale per 16 channels) + 170 B indexer = **890 B/token**, re-derived independently in [deepseek41f/architecture.md §5.1](../models/deepseek41f/architecture.md) and [flash-attention.md §11](../cross-cutting/flash-attention.md). DeepSeek's own report is explicit that this FP4 *"reduces storage rather than accelerates matrix multiplication"*, so the **format** needs no FP4 tensor core — that is the true part of the "not Blackwell-only" claim.
* **The layout is only *executed* where vLLM's `nvfp4_ds_mla` KV dtype resolves.** That dtype resolves only through `FLASHMLA_MEGA_ATTN_DSV41`, gated on `capability.major == 10` → **sm_100 / sm_103 only: B200, B300, GB300** ([flash-attention.md §11](../cross-cutting/flash-attention.md): *"nvfp4_ds_mla resolves only through FLASHMLA_MEGA_ATTN_DSV41 … B200/B300/GB300 get the 890 B/token cache; H100/H200, RTX PRO 6000 and MI355X do not"*; [quantization-formats.md §9.1](../cross-cutting/quantization-formats.md): *"890 B/token is the FP4-KV figure and it presumes the Blackwell FP4-KV kernel"*).
* **Everywhere else, budget the same layout at FP8 = 1,650 B/token** — [gpus/h200.md §2](../gpus/h200.md), [gpus/rtx6000-pro.md §9g](../gpus/rtx6000-pro.md), [gpus/mi355x.md §2](../gpus/mi355x.md) (*"no gfx950 kernel"*), [deepseek41f/rtx6000-pro §2](../models/deepseek41f/rtx6000-pro.md) (*"vLLM's SM12x dtype list still refuses NVFP4"*).
* **On H100, budget BF16 = 3,200 B/token.** [deepseek41f/h100 §1.4](../models/deepseek41f/h100.md) solves the measured `kv_cache_pool_tokens = 7,957,495` three ways and only BF16 leaves a plausible activation workspace (9.70 GB; FP8 would need 22.03 GB and FP4 28.08 GB). That is **the only empirical determination of this quantity in the tree, and it outranks the roofline**; its B200 cross-check (a 29,372,139-token pool that fits only at 890 B) confirms the gate from the other side, same engine and same week.
* **Both citations, per the cite-both rule.** The contrary source is [deepseek41f/architecture.md §5.2](../models/deepseek41f/architecture.md)'s *"works identically on Hopper and Ampere — no FP4 tensor cores needed"* (now annotated in place: true of the format, silent about the kernel) and the FlashMLA README's SM90+SM100 sparse-kernel listing — which covers **`FLASHMLA_SPARSE_DSV41`**, a different backend that does *not* carry the `nvfp4_ds_mla` dtype. The two statements are compatible once "layout" and "kernel" are kept apart.

**What moved.** [METHODOLOGY §8](../METHODOLOGY.md) (both DeepSeek rows), [deepseek41fnvfp4/h100](../models/deepseek41fnvfp4/h100.md) §0/§1 (headline fit and max-concurrency recut at 3,200 B, 890 B kept as an "if the gate opens" sensitivity), §1 and §2 of this matrix, [gpu-optimizations §4.3](./gpu-optimizations.md), [`pairs.json`](./pairs.json) (`deepseek41fnvfp4/h100`: 2,598 → **898** @8K, 217 → **61** @128K) and [gpus/h100.md §2](../gpus/h100.md) / [gpus/h200.md §2](../gpus/h200.md), where the FP8/BF16 row is now primary.

**What stays open (narrowed):** FlashInfer PR #4955 merged **2026-09-07** and ships a genuine SM120/SM121 NVFP4 sparse-MLA kernel, but vLLM's `FLASHINFER_MLA_SPARSE_DSV41` SM12x branch still requires `fp8`/`fp8_e4m3`/`fp8_ds_mla`, so the kernel is unreachable from vLLM ([flash-attention.md §11 and open question 16](../cross-cutting/flash-attention.md)). **⚠️ TO BE VERIFIED per engine version:** whether a newer vLLM nightly or SGLang exposes it, which would move RTX PRO 6000 (and only RTX PRO 6000) to 890 B/token.

### 6.4 Does the DSA sparse decode kernel exist on SM90 at all?

[deepseek41f/h100](../models/deepseek41f/h100.md): FlashMLA's README says V4.1 **sparse DECODE is SM100-only**, which conflicts with vLLM's own gate (`capability.major in [9, 10]`) *and* with working H100 runs. Losing the `FLASHMLA_MEGA_ATTN_DSV41` fusion is the leading suspect for the measured **2.11× roofline gap** on that pair. Open.

### 6.5 Does MXFP4 execute natively on sm_120, or fall back to Marlin?

[deepseek41f/rtx6000-pro](../models/deepseek41f/rtx6000-pro.md) states the dispute verbatim: [METHODOLOGY §8](../METHODOLOGY.md) and [gpus/rtx6000-pro.md §9g](../gpus/rtx6000-pro.md) say MXFP4 falls back to **Marlin W4A16 dequant** on stock vLLM; [quantization-formats.md §4.2/§9.6](../cross-cutting/quantization-formats.md) and *both* measured community builds say **native `b12x_fused_moe`**. Both are true of different builds; **no A/B has been published.**

### 6.6 How many RTX PRO 6000 cards does DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash need?

[gpus/rtx6000-pro.md §9g](../gpus/rtx6000-pro.md) computes `510.29e9 / (86.40e9 − 4e9) = 6.19` → **"at least 8 cards"**. [deepseek41f/rtx6000-pro §1.1](../models/deepseek41f/rtx6000-pro.md) shows that holds only with the Engram tables in HBM; with the host/NVMe offload that vLLM's own recipe makes mandatory elsewhere, resident weights fall to 307.53 GB → **4 cards**. Both published measurements of this pair run on **four**. This matrix uses 4 and cites both.

### 6.7 On Kimi-K3 / RTX PRO 6000, concurrency is not a KV number

[kimik3/rtx6000-pro §1.4](../models/kimik3/rtx6000-pro.md): the 1.46 M-token FP8 pool would hold **178** sequences at 8K, but the engine admits **12**. The binding constraint is CUDA-graph capture pools, B12X MoE scratch and AttnRes prefill workspace competing for the last ≈2 GB per card — recorded verbatim as an `unsupported` profile. The 12 / 11 figures in grid §2 are therefore **engine caps, not capacity**, and are not comparable to the KV-derived numbers in the other 39 cells.

### 6.8 `kimik3/b300`'s 8K and 128K figures have different bases

[kimik3/b300](../models/kimik3/b300.md): **8K = 101** is a *measured SGLang admission cap* (MXFP4-NOSPEC); **128K = 64** is an *estimate* at `--mem-fraction-static 0.85`, DCP8, FP8 KV, `S=5`. The corresponding estimate at 8K is 110, and the same lever set gives 68 (DSpark, `S=8`) or 250 (bf16 SSM state, `S=4`). The doc's own instruction is *"take 101 / 64 as the planning pair"*.

### 6.9 Engram sharding on ROCm — ⚠️ TO BE VERIFIED

[deepseek41f/mi355x §1.3](../models/deepseek41f/mi355x.md) assumes the Engram tables shard by row (≈50.69 GB/GPU at TP4), on the strength of a DGX Spark TP4 community run reporting *"48 GB of rows per worker"*. **No ROCm document states the Engram sharding policy.** If they replicate instead, `127.57 + 3 × 50.69 = 279.6 GB > 259.2 GB usable` and **TP4 becomes infeasible** — the whole MI355X recommendation flips to TP8 or Engram host-offload.

### 6.10 `qwen3827b/a100` is marked unsupported but is described as runnable

`supported_now: false` with `confidence: estimate`, and the headline is *"Runnable on paper, verified by nobody — no engine publishes an A100 profile (`recipes.vllm.ai/…/hw/a100.json` is 404)"*. The blocker is a missing profile plus [vllm#55008](../models/qwen3827b/a100.md) (open) selecting CUTLASS FP8 on SM80 and asserting — not a hardware limit. Graded ⚠️ here rather than ❌, unlike the three `not-runnable` pairs.

### 6.11 Smaller open items carried up from the pair docs

* **Marlin-2B resident weights:** 4.426 GB (tie honoured) vs 5.444 GB (materialised `lm_head`). ⚠️ which one vLLM actually allocates is unverified ([marlin2b/h200](../models/marlin2b/h200.md)).
* **Qwen3.8-27B NVFP4 checkpoint size:** [METHODOLOGY §8](../METHODOLOGY.md) pins **21.92 GB** (the `nvidia`/`RadixArk` export) but vLLM's `b200` recipe points at `Inferact/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4` at **26.38 GB** — 4.46 GB larger, ≈3 % less KV budget ([qwen3827b/b200](../models/qwen3827b/b200.md)).
* **A100 usable-HBM basis:** 80 GB decimal vs 80 GiB, 7.4 % apart, propagating into every A100 concurrency figure (§3).
* **GDN / KDA state dtype:** fp32 vs bf16 is engine-selectable and unmeasured for these models; it moves Qwen3.8-27B capacity by ≈2× and Kimi-K3 by +127 % (§5.2). ⚠️
* **`deepseek41f/gb300` mode mismatch:** the verdict recommends **4 GPUs fully resident** (128.96 GB/GPU) but the headline concurrency (16,164 / 1,378) comes from the **Engram-on-Grace** table (78.27 GB/GPU). The resident KV budget is 114.14 GB, not 164.83.
* **`unverified_count`** in `pairs.json` ranges from **11** (`deepseek41fnvfp4/h100`) to **30** (`deepseek41f/mi355x`); of the 40 pairs **5** are `measured`, **31** `estimate`, **3** `not-runnable` and **1** `speculative`.
